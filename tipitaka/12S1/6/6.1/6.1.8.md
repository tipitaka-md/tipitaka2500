# 6.1.8 Katamodakatissasutta

* Brahmasaṃyutta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Katamodakatissasutta

(179.)

1072\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. Tena kho pana samayena bhagavā divāvihāragato hoti paṭisallīno. Atha kho subrahmā ca paccekabrahmā suddhāvāso ca paccekabrahmā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkamiṁsu; upasaṅkamitvā paccekaṁ dvārabāhaṁ nissāya aṭṭhaṁsu. Atha kho suddhāvāso paccekabrahmā katamodakatissakaṁ bhikkhuṁ ārabbha bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

1073\. _“Appameyyaṁ paminanto,_  
_Kodha vidvā vikappaye;_  
_Appameyyaṁ pamāyinaṁ,_  
_Nivutaṁ taṁ maññe akissavan”ti._
