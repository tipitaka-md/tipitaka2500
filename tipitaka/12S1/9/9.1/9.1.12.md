# 9.1.12 Majjhanhikasutta

* Vanasaṃyutta

* Vanavagga

* Majjhanhikasutta

(232.)

1428\. Ekaṁ samayaṁ aññataro bhikkhu kosalesu viharati aññatarasmiṁ vanasaṇḍe. Atha kho tasmiṁ vanasaṇḍe adhivatthā devatā yena so bhikkhu tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā tassa bhikkhuno santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

1429\. _“Ṭhite majjhanhike kāle,_  
_sannisīvesu pakkhisu;_  
_Saṇateva brahāraññaṁ,_  
_taṁ bhayaṁ paṭibhāti maṁ._  

1430\. _Ṭhite majjhanhike kāle,_  
_sannisīvesu pakkhisu;_  
_Saṇateva brahāraññaṁ,_  
_sā rati paṭibhāti man”ti._
