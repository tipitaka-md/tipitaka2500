# 4.1.9 Paṭhamaāyusutta

* Mārasaṃyutta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Paṭhamaāyusutta

(145.)

808\. Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati veḷuvane kalandakanivāpe. Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi— “bhikkhavo”ti. “Bhadante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ. Bhagavā etadavoca—

809\. “Appamidaṁ, bhikkhave, manussānaṁ āyu. Gamanīyo samparāyo, kattabbaṁ kusalaṁ, caritabbaṁ brahmacariyaṁ. Natthi jātassa amaraṇaṁ. Yo, bhikkhave, ciraṁ jīvati, so vassasataṁ appaṁ vā bhiyyo”ti.

810\. Atha kho māro pāpimā yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi—

811\. _“Dīghamāyu manussānaṁ,_  
_na naṁ hīḷe suporiso;_  
_Careyya khīramattova,_  
_natthi maccussa āgamo”ti._  

812\. _“Appamāyu manussānaṁ,_  
_hīḷeyya naṁ suporiso;_  
_Careyyādittasīsova,_  
_natthi maccussa nāgamo”ti._  

813\. Atha kho māro…pe… tatthevantaradhāyīti.
