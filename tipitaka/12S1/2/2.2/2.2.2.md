# 2.2.2 Veṇḍusutta

* Devaputtasaṃyutta

* Anāthapiṇḍikavagga

* Veṇḍusutta

(93.)

433\. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho veṇḍu devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

434\. _“Sukhitāva te manujā,_  
_sugataṁ payirupāsiya;_  
_Yuñjaṁ gotamasāsane,_  
_appamattā nu sikkhare”ti._  

435\. _“Ye me pavutte siṭṭhipade, (veṇḍūti bhagavā)_  
_Anusikkhanti jhāyino;_  
_Kāle te appamajjantā,_  
_Na maccuvasagā siyun”ti._
