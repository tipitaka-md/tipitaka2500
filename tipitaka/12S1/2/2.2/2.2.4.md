# 2.2.4 Nandanasutta

* Devaputtasaṃyutta

* Anāthapiṇḍikavagga

* Nandanasutta

(95.)

438\. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho nandano devaputto bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi—

439\. _“Pucchāmi taṁ gotama bhūripañña,_  
_Anāvaṭaṁ bhagavato ñāṇadassanaṁ;_  
_Kathaṁvidhaṁ sīlavantaṁ vadanti,_  
_Kathaṁvidhaṁ paññavantaṁ vadanti;_  
_Kathaṁvidho dukkhamaticca iriyati,_  
_Kathaṁvidhaṁ devatā pūjayantī”ti._  

440\. _“Yo sīlavā paññavā bhāvitatto,_  
_Samāhito jhānarato satīmā;_  
_Sabbassa sokā vigatā pahīnā,_  
_Khīṇāsavo antimadehadhārī._  

441\. _Tathāvidhaṁ sīlavantaṁ vadanti,_  
_Tathāvidhaṁ paññavantaṁ vadanti;_  
_Tathāvidho dukkhamaticca iriyati,_  
_Tathāvidhaṁ devatā pūjayantī”ti._
