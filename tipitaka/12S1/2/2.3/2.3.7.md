# 2.3.7 Nandasutta

* Devaputtasaṃyutta

* Nānātitthiyavagga

* Nandasutta

(108.)

535\. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho nando devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

536\. _“Accenti kālā tarayanti rattiyo,_  
_Vayoguṇā anupubbaṁ jahanti;_  
_Etaṁ bhayaṁ maraṇe pekkhamāno,_  
_Puññāni kayirātha sukhāvahānī”ti._  

537\. _“Accenti kālā tarayanti rattiyo,_  
_Vayoguṇā anupubbaṁ jahanti;_  
_Etaṁ bhayaṁ maraṇe pekkhamāno,_  
_Lokāmisaṁ pajahe santipekkho”ti._
