# 2.1.7 Pañcālacaṇḍasutta

* Devaputtasaṃyutta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Pañcālacaṇḍasutta

(88.)

395\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. Ekamantaṁ ṭhito kho pañcālacaṇḍo devaputto bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

396\. _“Sambādhe vata okāsaṁ,_  
_Avindi bhūrimedhaso;_  
_Yo jhānamabujjhi buddho,_  
_Paṭilīnanisabho munī”ti._  

397\. _“Sambādhe vāpi vindanti, (pañcālacaṇḍāti bhagavā)_  
_Dhammaṁ nibbānapattiyā;_  
_Ye satiṁ paccalatthaṁsu,_  
_Sammā te susamāhitā”ti._
