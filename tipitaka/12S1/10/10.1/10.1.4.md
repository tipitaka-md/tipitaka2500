# 10.1.4 Maṇibhaddasutta

* Yakkhasaṃyutta

* Indakavagga

* Maṇibhaddasutta

(238.)

1465\. Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā magadhesu viharati maṇimālike cetiye maṇibhaddassa yakkhassa bhavane. Atha kho maṇibhaddo yakkho yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavato santike imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

1466\. _“Satīmato sadā bhaddaṁ,_  
_satimā sukhamedhati;_  
_Satīmato suve seyyo,_  
_verā ca parimuccatī”ti._  

1467\. _“Satīmato sadā bhaddaṁ,_  
_satimā sukhamedhati;_  
_Satīmato suve seyyo,_  
_verā na parimuccati._  

1468\. _Yassa sabbamahorattaṁ,_  
_ahiṁsāya rato mano;_  
_Mettaṁso sabbabhūtesu,_  
_veraṁ tassa na kenacī”ti._
