# 10.1.2 Sakkanāmasutta

* Yakkhasaṃyutta

* Indakavagga

* Sakkanāmasutta

(236.)

1455\. Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati gijjhakūṭe pabbate. Atha kho sakkanāmako yakkho yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi—

1456\. _“Sabbaganthappahīnassa,_  
_vippamuttassa te sato;_  
_Samaṇassa na taṁ sādhu,_  
_yadaññamanusāsasī”ti._  

1457\. _“Yena kenaci vaṇṇena,_  
_saṁvāso sakka jāyati;_  
_Na taṁ arahati sappañño,_  
_manasā anukampituṁ._  

1458\. _Manasā ce pasannena,_  
_yadaññamanusāsati;_  
_Na tena hoti saṁyutto,_  
_yānukampā anuddayā”ti._
