# 10.1.6 Piyaṅkarasutta

* Yakkhasaṃyutta

* Indakavagga

* Piyaṅkarasutta

(240.)

1479\. Ekaṁ samayaṁ āyasmā anuruddho sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena āyasmā anuruddho rattiyā paccūsasamayaṁ paccuṭṭhāya dhammapadāni bhāsati. Atha kho piyaṅkaramātā yakkhinī puttakaṁ evaṁ tosesi—

1480\. _“Mā saddaṁ kari piyaṅkara,_  
_Bhikkhu dhammapadāni bhāsati;_  
_Api ca dhammapadaṁ vijāniya,_  
_Paṭipajjema hitāya no siyā._  

1481\. _Pāṇesu ca saṁyamāmase,_  
_Sampajānamusā na bhaṇāmase;_  
_Sikkhema susīlyamattano,_  
_Api muccema pisācayoniyā”ti._
