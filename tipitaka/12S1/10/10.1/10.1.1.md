# 10.1.1 Indakasutta

* Yakkhasaṃyutta

* Indakavagga

* Indakasutta

(235.)

1451\. Evaṁ me sutaṁ— ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati indakūṭe pabbate, indakassa yakkhassa bhavane. Atha kho indako yakkho yena bhagavā tenupasaṅkami; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṁ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi—

1452\. _“Rūpaṁ na jīvanti vadanti buddhā,_  
_Kathaṁ nvayaṁ vindatimaṁ sarīraṁ;_  
_Kutassa aṭṭhīyakapiṇḍameti,_  
_Kathaṁ nvayaṁ sajjati gabbharasmin”ti._  

1453\. _“Paṭhamaṁ kalalaṁ hoti,_  
_kalalā hoti abbudaṁ;_  
_Abbudā jāyate pesi,_  
_pesi nibbattatī ghano;_  
_Ghanā pasākhā jāyanti,_  
_kesā lomā nakhāpi ca._  

1454\. _Yañcassa bhuñjatī mātā,_  
_annaṁ pānañca bhojanaṁ;_  
_Tena so tattha yāpeti,_  
_mātukucchigato naro”ti._
