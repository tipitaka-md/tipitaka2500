# 1.5.3 Annasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Ādittavagga

* Annasutta

(43.)

246\. _“Annamevābhinandanti,_  
_ubhaye devamānusā;_  
_Atha ko nāma so yakkho,_  
_yaṁ annaṁ nābhinandatī”ti._  

247\. _“Ye naṁ dadanti saddhāya,_  
_vippasannena cetasā;_  
_Tameva annaṁ bhajati,_  
_asmiṁ loke paramhi ca._  

248\. _Tasmā vineyya maccheraṁ,_  
_dajjā dānaṁ malābhibhū;_  
_Puññāni paralokasmiṁ,_  
_patiṭṭhā honti pāṇinan”ti._
