# 1.5.8 Jetavanasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Ādittavagga

* Jetavanasutta

(48.)

258\. _“Idañhi taṁ jetavanaṁ,_  
_isisaṁghanisevitaṁ;_  
_Āvutthaṁ dhammarājena,_  
_pītisañjananaṁ mama._  

259\. _Kammaṁ vijjā ca dhammo ca,_  
_sīlaṁ jīvitamuttamaṁ;_  
_Etena maccā sujjhanti,_  
_na gottena dhanena vā._  

260\. _Tasmā hi paṇḍito poso,_  
_sampassaṁ atthamattano;_  
_Yoniso vicine dhammaṁ,_  
_evaṁ tattha visujjhati._  

261\. _Sāriputtova paññāya,_  
_sīlena upasamena ca;_  
_Yopi pāraṅgato bhikkhu,_  
_etāvaparamo siyā”ti._
