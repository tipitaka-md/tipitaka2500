# 1.5.5 Anomasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Ādittavagga

* Anomasutta

(45.)

250\. _“Anomanāmaṁ nipuṇatthadassiṁ,_  
_Paññādadaṁ kāmālaye asattaṁ;_  
_Taṁ passatha sabbaviduṁ sumedhaṁ,_  
_Ariye pathe kamamānaṁ mahesin”ti._
