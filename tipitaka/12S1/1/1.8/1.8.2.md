# 1.8.2 Rathasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Chetvāvagga

* Rathasutta

(72.)

337\. _“Kiṁsu rathassa paññāṇaṁ,_  
_kiṁsu paññāṇamaggino;_  
_Kiṁsu raṭṭhassa paññāṇaṁ,_  
_kiṁsu paññāṇamitthiyā”ti._  

338\. _“Dhajo rathassa paññāṇaṁ,_  
_dhūmo paññāṇamaggino;_  
_Rājā raṭṭhassa paññāṇaṁ,_  
_bhattā paññāṇamitthiyā”ti._
