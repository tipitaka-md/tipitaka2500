# 1.8.9 Pātheyyasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Chetvāvagga

* Pātheyyasutta

(79.)

357\. _“Kiṁsu bandhati pātheyyaṁ,_  
_kiṁsu bhogānamāsayo;_  
_Kiṁsu naraṁ parikassati,_  
_kiṁsu lokasmi dujjahaṁ;_  
_Kismiṁ baddhā puthū sattā,_  
_pāsena sakuṇī yathā”ti._  

358\. _“Saddhā bandhati pātheyyaṁ,_  
_sirī bhogānamāsayo;_  
_Icchā naraṁ parikassati,_  
_icchā lokasmi dujjahā;_  
_Icchābaddhā puthū sattā,_  
_pāsena sakuṇī yathā”ti._
