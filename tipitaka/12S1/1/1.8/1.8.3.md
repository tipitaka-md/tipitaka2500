# 1.8.3 Vittasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Chetvāvagga

* Vittasutta

(73.)

339\. _“Kiṁsūdha vittaṁ purisassa seṭṭhaṁ,_  
_Kiṁsu suciṇṇo sukhamāvahati;_  
_Kiṁsu have sādutaraṁ rasānaṁ,_  
_Kathaṁjīviṁ jīvitamāhu seṭṭhan”ti._  

340\. _“Saddhīdha vittaṁ purisassa seṭṭhaṁ,_  
_Dhammo suciṇṇo sukhamāvahati;_  
_Saccaṁ have sādutaraṁ rasānaṁ,_  
_Paññājīviṁ jīvitamāhu seṭṭhan”ti._
