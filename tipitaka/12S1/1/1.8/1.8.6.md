# 1.8.6 Najīratisutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Chetvāvagga

* Najīratisutta

(76.)

346\. _“Kiṁ jīrati kiṁ na jīrati,_  
_kiṁsu uppathoti vuccati;_  
_Kiṁsu dhammānaṁ paripantho,_  
_kiṁsu rattindivakkhayo;_  
_Kiṁ malaṁ brahmacariyassa,_  
_kiṁ sinānamanodakaṁ._  

347\. _Kati lokasmiṁ chiddāni,_  
_yattha vittaṁ na tiṭṭhati;_  
_Bhagavantaṁ puṭṭhumāgamma,_  
_kathaṁ jānemu taṁ mayan”ti._  

348\. _“Rūpaṁ jīrati maccānaṁ,_  
_nāmagottaṁ na jīrati;_  
_Rāgo uppathoti vuccati._  

349\. _Lobho dhammānaṁ paripantho,_  
_Vayo rattindivakkhayo;_  
_Itthī malaṁ brahmacariyassa,_  
_Etthāyaṁ sajjate pajā;_  
_Tapo ca brahmacariyañca,_  
_Taṁ sinānamanodakaṁ._  

350\. _Cha lokasmiṁ chiddāni,_  
_yattha vittaṁ na tiṭṭhati;_  
_Ālasyañca pamādo ca,_  
_anuṭṭhānaṁ asaṁyamo;_  
_Niddā tandī ca te chidde,_  
_sabbaso taṁ vivajjaye”ti._
