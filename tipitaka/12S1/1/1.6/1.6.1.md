# 1.6.1 Jarāsutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Jarāvagga

* Jarāsutta

(51.)

288\. _“Kiṁsu yāva jarā sādhu,_  
_kiṁsu sādhu patiṭṭhitaṁ;_  
_Kiṁsu narānaṁ ratanaṁ,_  
_kiṁsu corehi dūharan”ti._  

289\. _“Sīlaṁ yāva jarā sādhu,_  
_saddhā sādhu patiṭṭhitā;_  
_Paññā narānaṁ ratanaṁ,_  
_puññaṁ corehi dūharan”ti._
