# 1.6.2 Ajarasāsutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Jarāvagga

* Ajarasāsutta

(52.)

290\. _“Kiṁsu ajarasā sādhu,_  
_kiṁsu sādhu adhiṭṭhitaṁ;_  
_Kiṁsu narānaṁ ratanaṁ,_  
_kiṁsu corehyahāriyan”ti._  

291\. _“Sīlaṁ ajarasā sādhu,_  
_saddhā sādhu adhiṭṭhitā;_  
_Paññā narānaṁ ratanaṁ,_  
_puññaṁ corehyahāriyan”ti._
