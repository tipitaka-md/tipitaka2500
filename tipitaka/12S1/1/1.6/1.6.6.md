# 1.6.6 Dutiyajanasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Jarāvagga

* Dutiyajanasutta

(56.)

298\. _“Kiṁsu janeti purisaṁ,_  
_kiṁsu tassa vidhāvati;_  
_Kiṁsu saṁsāramāpādi,_  
_kismā na parimuccatī”ti._  

299\. _“Taṇhā janeti purisaṁ,_  
_cittamassa vidhāvati;_  
_Satto saṁsāramāpādi,_  
_dukkhā na parimuccatī”ti._
