# 1.6.7 Tatiyajanasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Jarāvagga

* Tatiyajanasutta

(57.)

300\. _“Kiṁsu janeti purisaṁ,_  
_kiṁsu tassa vidhāvati;_  
_Kiṁsu saṁsāramāpādi,_  
_kiṁsu tassa parāyanan”ti._  

301\. _“Taṇhā janeti purisaṁ,_  
_cittamassa vidhāvati;_  
_Satto saṁsāramāpādi,_  
_kammaṁ tassa parāyanan”ti._
