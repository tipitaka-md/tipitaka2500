# 1.7.4 Saṃyojanasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Addhavagga

* Saṃyojanasutta

(64.)

317\. _“Kiṁsu saṁyojano loko,_  
_kiṁsu tassa vicāraṇaṁ;_  
_Kissassu vippahānena,_  
_nibbānaṁ iti vuccatī”ti._  

318\. _“Nandīsaṁyojano loko,_  
_vitakkassa vicāraṇaṁ;_  
_Taṇhāya vippahānena,_  
_nibbānaṁ iti vuccatī”ti._
