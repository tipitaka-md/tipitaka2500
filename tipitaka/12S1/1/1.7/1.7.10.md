# 1.7.10 Lokasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Addhavagga

* Lokasutta

(70.)

329\. _“Kismiṁ loko samuppanno,_  
_kismiṁ kubbati santhavaṁ;_  
_Kissa loko upādāya,_  
_kismiṁ loko vihaññatī”ti._  

330\. _“Chasu loko samuppanno,_  
_chasu kubbati santhavaṁ;_  
_Channameva upādāya,_  
_chasu loko vihaññatī”ti._  

331\. Addhavaggo sattamo.

332\. 

333\. _Nāmaṁ cittañca taṇhā ca,_  
_saṁyojanañca bandhanā;_  
_Abbhāhatuḍḍito pihito,_  
_icchā lokena te dasāti._
