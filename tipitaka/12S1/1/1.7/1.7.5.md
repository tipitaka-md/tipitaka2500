# 1.7.5 Bandhanasutta

* Devatāsaṃyutta

* Addhavagga

* Bandhanasutta

(65.)

319\. _“Kiṁsu sambandhano loko,_  
_kiṁsu tassa vicāraṇaṁ;_  
_Kissassu vippahānena,_  
_sabbaṁ chindati bandhanan”ti._  

320\. _“Nandīsambandhano loko,_  
_vitakkassa vicāraṇaṁ;_  
_Taṇhāya vippahānena,_  
_sabbaṁ chindati bandhanan”ti._
