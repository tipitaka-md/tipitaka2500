# 2.2.12

* Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

* Sukhavagga

(76.)

156\. “Dvemāni, bhikkhave, sukhāni. Katamāni dve? Sātārammaṇañca sukhaṁ upekkhārammaṇañca sukhaṁ. Imāni kho, bhikkhave, dve sukhāni. Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, imesaṁ dvinnaṁ sukhānaṁ yadidaṁ upekkhārammaṇaṁ sukhan”ti.
