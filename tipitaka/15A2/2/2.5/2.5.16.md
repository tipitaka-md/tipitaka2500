# 2.5.16

* Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

* Bālavagga

(114.)

197\. “Dvinnaṁ, bhikkhave, āsavā na vaḍḍhanti. Katamesaṁ dvinnaṁ? Yo ca āpattiyā āpattisaññī, yo ca anāpattiyā anāpattisaññī. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, dvinnaṁ āsavā na vaḍḍhantī”ti.
