# 2.4.1.22 Sanidassanattika

* Niddesa

* Aṭṭhakathākaṇḍa

* Tikaatthuddhāra

* Sanidassanattika

(1438.)

2144\. Katame dhammā sanidassanasappaṭighā? Rūpāyatanaṁ— ime dhammā sanidassanasappaṭighā.

(1439.)

2145\. Katame dhammā anidassanasappaṭighā? Cakkhāyatanaṁ…pe… phoṭṭhabbāyatanaṁ— ime dhammā anidassanasappaṭighā.

(1440.)

2146\. Katame dhammā anidassanaappaṭighā? Catūsu bhūmīsu kusalaṁ, akusalaṁ, catūsu bhūmīsu vipāko, tīsu bhūmīsu kiriyābyākataṁ, yañca rūpaṁ anidassanaṁ appaṭighaṁ dhammāyatanapariyāpannaṁ, nibbānañca— ime dhammā anidassanaappaṭighā.

2147\. Tikaṁ.
