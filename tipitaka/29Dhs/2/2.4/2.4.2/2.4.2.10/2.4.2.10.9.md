# 2.4.2.10.9 Cittasaṃsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānaduka

* Niddesa

* Aṭṭhakathākaṇḍa

* Dukaatthuddhāra

* Mahantaraduka

* Cittasaṃsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānaduka

(1541.)

2252\. Katame dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānā? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho— ime dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānā.

(1542.)

2253\. Katame dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānā? Cittañca, rūpañca, nibbānañca— ime dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānā.
