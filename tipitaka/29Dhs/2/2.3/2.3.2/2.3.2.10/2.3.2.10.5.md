# 2.3.2.10.5 Cittasaṃsaṭṭhaduka

* Niddesa

* Nikkhepakaṇḍa

* Dukanikkhepa

* Mahantaraduka

* Cittasaṃsaṭṭhaduka

(1199.)

1895\. Katame dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhā? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho— ime dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhā.

(1200.)

1896\. Katame dhammā cittavisaṁsaṭṭhā? Sabbañca rūpaṁ, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu— ime dhammā cittavisaṁsaṭṭhā. Cittaṁ na vattabbaṁ— cittena saṁsaṭṭhantipi, cittena visaṁsaṭṭhantipi.
