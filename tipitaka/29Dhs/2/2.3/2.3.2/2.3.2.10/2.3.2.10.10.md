# 2.3.2.10.10 Cittasaṃsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhūduka

* Niddesa

* Nikkhepakaṇḍa

* Dukanikkhepa

* Mahantaraduka

* Cittasaṃsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhūduka

(1209.)

1905\. Katame dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuno? Vedanākkhandho, saññākkhandho, saṅkhārakkhandho— ime dhammā cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuno.

(1210.)

1906\. Katame dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuno? Cittañca, sabbañca rūpaṁ, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu— ime dhammā no cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuno.
