# 2.3.2.12.6 Kilesasaṃkiliṭṭhaduka

* Niddesa

* Nikkhepakaṇḍa

* Dukanikkhepa

* Kilesagocchaka

* Kilesasaṃkiliṭṭhaduka

(1255.)

1954\. Katame dhammā kilesā ceva saṁkiliṭṭhā ca? Teva kilesā kilesā ceva saṁkiliṭṭhā ca.

(1256.)

1955\. Katame dhammā saṁkiliṭṭhā ceva no ca kilesā? Tehi dhammehi ye dhammā saṁkiliṭṭhā, te dhamme ṭhapetvā vedanākkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho— ime dhammā saṁkiliṭṭhā ceva no ca kilesā.
