# 2.3.2.2.4 Sappaṭighaduka

* Niddesa

* Nikkhepakaṇḍa

* Dukanikkhepa

* Cūḷantaraduka

* Sappaṭighaduka

(1095.)

1764\. Katame dhammā sappaṭighā? Cakkhāyatanaṁ…pe… phoṭṭhabbāyatanaṁ— ime dhammā sappaṭighā.

(1096.)

1765\. Katame dhammā appaṭighā? Vedanākkhandho…pe… viññāṇakkhandho, yañca rūpaṁ anidassanaṁ appaṭighaṁ dhammāyatanapariyāpannaṁ, asaṅkhatā ca dhātu— ime dhammā appaṭighā.
