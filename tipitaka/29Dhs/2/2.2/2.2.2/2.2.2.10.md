# 2.2.2.10 Dasaka

* Niddesa

* Rūpakaṇḍa

* Mātikā

* Dasaka

(592.)

1228\. Dasavidhena rūpasaṅgaho—

1229\. Cakkhundriyaṁ, sotindriyaṁ, ghānindriyaṁ, jivhindriyaṁ kāyindriyaṁ, itthindriyaṁ, purisindriyaṁ, jīvitindriyaṁ, na indriyaṁ rūpaṁ atthi sappaṭighaṁ, atthi appaṭighaṁ.

1230\. Evaṁ dasavidhena rūpasaṅgaho. Dasakaṁ.
