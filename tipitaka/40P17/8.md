# 8 Kusalattika, Dassanahetuttika

* Kusalattika, Dassanahetuttika

(20.)

50\. Akusalaṁ dassanena pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naakusalo nadassanena pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

51\. Akusalaṁ bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naakusalo nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

52\. Akusalaṁ nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nakusalo nanevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

53\. Abyākataṁ nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā naabyākato nanevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
