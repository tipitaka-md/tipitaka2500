# 24 Saṃkiliṭṭhattika, Kusalattika

* Saṃkiliṭṭhattika, Kusalattika

(41.)

112\. Asaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesikaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesiko nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

113\. Asaṁkiliṭṭhaasaṁkilesikaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naasaṁkiliṭṭhaasaṁkilesiko nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha, adhipatiyā cha…pe… avigate cha.

114\. Saṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesikaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesiko naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

115\. Asaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesikaṁ abyākataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā naasaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesiko naabyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha…pe… avigate cha.
