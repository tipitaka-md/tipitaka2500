# 14 Kusalattika, Micchattaniyatattika

* Kusalattika, Micchattaniyatattika

(26.)

69\. Akusalaṁ micchattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naakusalo namicchattaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

70\. Kusalaṁ sammattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nakusalo nasammattaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

71\. Abyākataṁ aniyataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā naabyākato naaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā pañca…pe… avigate pañca.
