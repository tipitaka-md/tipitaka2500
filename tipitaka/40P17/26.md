# 26 Pītittika, Kusalattika

* Pītittika, Kusalattika

(43.)

119\. Pītisahagataṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca napītisahagato nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… satta.

120\. Sukhasahagataṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasukhasahagato nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… satta.

121\. Upekkhāsahagataṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naupekkhāsahagato nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… satta.

122\. Hetuyā ekavīsati, adhipatiyā ekavīsati…pe… avigate ekavīsati. (Vedanāttikasadisaṁ. Saṁkhittaṁ.)
