# 17 Āsavasampayuttaduka, Hetuduka

* Āsavasampayuttaduka, Hetuduka

(55.)

130\. Āsavasampayuttaṁ hetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavasampayutto nahetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

131\. Āsavavippayuttaṁ hetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavavippayutto nahetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

132\. Āsavasampayuttaṁ hetuñca āsavavippayuttaṁ hetuñca dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavasampayutto nahetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā nava, ārammaṇe tīṇi…pe… vipāke ekaṁ…pe… avigate nava.

133\. Āsavasampayuttaṁ nahetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavasampayutto nanahetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

134\. Āsavavippayuttaṁ nahetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavasampayutto nanahetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cattāri…pe… vipāke ekaṁ…pe… avigate cattāri.
