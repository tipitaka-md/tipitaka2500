# 2.1.2 Sukhasutta

* Vedanāsaṃyutta

* Sagāthāvagga

* Sukhasutta

(250.)

1098\. “Tisso imā, bhikkhave, vedanā. Katamā tisso? Sukhā vedanā, dukkhā vedanā, adukkhamasukhā vedanā— imā kho, bhikkhave, tisso vedanāti.

1099\. _Sukhaṁ vā yadi vā dukkhaṁ,_  
_adukkhamasukhaṁ saha;_  
_Ajjhattañca bahiddhā ca,_  
_yaṁ kiñci atthi veditaṁ._  

1100\. _Etaṁ dukkhanti ñatvāna,_  
_mosadhammaṁ palokinaṁ;_  
_Phussa phussa vayaṁ passaṁ,_  
_evaṁ tattha virajjatī”ti._  

1101\. Dutiyaṁ.
