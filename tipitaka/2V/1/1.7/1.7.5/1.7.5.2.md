# 1.7.5.2 Dutiyaanāhaṭasikkhāpada

* Mahāvibhaṅga

* Sekhiyakaṇḍa

* Kabaḷavagga

* Dutiyaanāhaṭasikkhāpada

(618.)

1894\. Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū bhuñjamānā sabbaṁ hatthaṁ mukhe pakkhipanti…pe… .

1895\. “Na bhuñjamāno sabbaṁ hatthaṁ mukhe pakkhipissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (42:187)

1896\. Na bhuñjamānena sabbo hattho mukhe pakkhipitabbo. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca bhuñjamāno sabbaṁ hatthaṁ mukhe pakkhipati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1897\. Anāpatti— asañcicca…pe… ādikammikassāti.

1898\. Dutiyasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
