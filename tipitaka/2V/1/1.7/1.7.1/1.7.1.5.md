# 1.7.1.5 Susaṃvutasikkhāpada

* Mahāvibhaṅga

* Sekhiyakaṇḍa

* Parimaṇḍalavagga

* Susaṃvutasikkhāpada

(580.)

1705\. Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū hatthampi pādampi kīḷāpentā antaraghare gacchanti…pe… .

1706\. “Susaṁvuto antaraghare gamissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (5:150)

1707\. Susaṁvutena antaraghare gantabbaṁ. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca hatthaṁ vā pādaṁ vā kīḷāpento antaraghare gacchati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1708\. Anāpatti— asañcicca, assatiyā, ajānantassa, gilānassa, ummattakassa, ādikammikassāti.

1709\. Pañcamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
