# 1.7.3.8 Pattasaññīpaṭiggahaṇasikkhāpada

* Mahāvibhaṅga

* Sekhiyakaṇḍa

* Khambhakatavagga

* Pattasaññīpaṭiggahaṇasikkhāpada

(603.)

1820\. Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū tahaṁ tahaṁ olokentā piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggaṇhanti, ākirantepi atikkantepi na jānanti…pe… .

1821\. “Pattasaññī piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggahessāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (28:173)

1822\. Pattasaññinā piṇḍapāto paṭiggahetabbo. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca tahaṁ tahaṁ olokento piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggaṇhāti, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1823\. Anāpatti— asañcicca…pe… ādikammikassāti.

1824\. Aṭṭhamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
