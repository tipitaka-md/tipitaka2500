# 1.7.6.9 Satthapāṇisikkhāpada

* Mahāvibhaṅga

* Sekhiyakaṇḍa

* Surusuruvagga

* Satthapāṇisikkhāpada

(636.)

1990\. Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū satthapāṇissa dhammaṁ desenti…pe… .

1991\. “Na satthapāṇissa agilānassa dhammaṁ desessāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (59:204)

1992\. Satthaṁ nāma ekatodhāraṁ ubhatodhāraṁ paharaṇaṁ.

1993\. Na satthapāṇissa agilānassa dhammo desetabbo. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca satthapāṇissa agilānassa dhammaṁ deseti, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1994\. Anāpatti— asañcicca…pe… ādikammikassāti.

1995\. Navamasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
