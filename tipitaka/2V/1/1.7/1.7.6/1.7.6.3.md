# 1.7.6.3 Pattanillehakasikkhāpada

* Mahāvibhaṅga

* Sekhiyakaṇḍa

* Surusuruvagga

* Pattanillehakasikkhāpada

(629.)

1950\. Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū pattanillehakaṁ bhuñjanti…pe… .

1951\. “Na pattanillehakaṁ bhuñjissāmīti sikkhā karaṇīyā”ti. (53:198)

1952\. Na pattanillehakaṁ bhuñjitabbaṁ. Yo anādariyaṁ paṭicca pattanillehakaṁ bhuñjati, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

1953\. Anāpatti— asañcicca, assatiyā, ajānantassa, gilānassa, parittake sese ekato saṅkaḍḍhitvā nillehitvā bhuñjati, āpadāsu, ummattakassa, ādikammikassāti.

1954\. Tatiyasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
