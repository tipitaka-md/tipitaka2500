# 1.5.8.6 Amūlakasikkhāpada

* Mahāvibhaṅga

* Pācittiyakaṇḍa

* Sahadhammikavagga

* Amūlakasikkhāpada

(459.)

1367\. Tena samayena buddho bhagavā sāvatthiyaṁ viharati jetavane anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. Tena kho pana samayena chabbaggiyā bhikkhū bhikkhuṁ amūlakena saṁghādisesena anuddhaṁsenti.

1368\. Ye te bhikkhū appicchā…pe… te ujjhāyanti khiyyanti vipācenti— “kathañhi nāma chabbaggiyā bhikkhū bhikkhuṁ amūlakena saṁghādisesena anuddhaṁsessantī”ti…pe… “saccaṁ kira tumhe, bhikkhave, bhikkhuṁ amūlakena saṁghādisesena anuddhaṁsethā”ti? “Saccaṁ, bhagavā”ti. Vigarahi buddho bhagavā…pe… kathañhi nāma tumhe, moghapurisā, bhikkhuṁ amūlakena saṁghādisesena anuddhaṁsessatha. Netaṁ, moghapurisā, appasannānaṁ vā pasādāya…pe… evañca pana, bhikkhave, imaṁ sikkhāpadaṁ uddiseyyātha—

(460.)

1369\. “Yo pana bhikkhu bhikkhuṁ amūlakena saṁghādisesena anuddhaṁseyya, pācittiyan”ti. (76:125)

(461.)

1370\. Yo panāti yo yādiso…pe… bhikkhūti…pe… ayaṁ imasmiṁ atthe adhippeto bhikkhūti.

1371\. Bhikkhunti aññaṁ bhikkhuṁ.

1372\. Amūlakaṁ nāma adiṭṭhaṁ assutaṁ aparisaṅkitaṁ.

1373\. Saṁghādisesenāti terasannaṁ aññatarena.

1374\. Anuddhaṁseyyāti codeti vā codāpeti vā, āpatti pācittiyassa.

(462.)

1375\. Upasampanne upasampannasaññī amūlakena saṁghādisesena anuddhaṁseti, āpatti pācittiyassa. Upasampanne vematiko amūlakena saṁghādisesena anuddhaṁseti, āpatti pācittiyassa. Upasampanne anupasampannasaññī amūlakena saṁghādisesena anuddhaṁseti, āpatti pācittiyassa.

1376\. Ācāravipattiyā vā diṭṭhivipattiyā vā anuddhaṁseti, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Anupasampannaṁ anuddhaṁseti, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Anupasampanne upasampannasaññī, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Anupasampanne vematiko, āpatti dukkaṭassa. Anupasampanne anupasampannasaññī, āpatti dukkaṭassa.

(463.)

1377\. Anāpatti— tathāsaññī codeti, vā codāpeti vā, ummattakassa, ādikammikassāti.

1378\. Amūlakasikkhāpadaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
