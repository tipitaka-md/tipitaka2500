# 32 Āsavaduka, Kusalattika

* Āsavaduka, Kusalattika

(41.)

119\. Noāsavaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca noāsavo nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.

120\. Āsavaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca noāsavo naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

121\. Noāsavaṁ abyākataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā nanoāsavo naabyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
