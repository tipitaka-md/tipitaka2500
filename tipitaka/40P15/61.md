# 61 Sukhasahagataduka, Kusalattika

* Sukhasahagataduka, Kusalattika

(70.)

193\. Sukhasahagataṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasukhasahagato nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Nasukhasahagataṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasukhasahagato nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Sukhasahagataṁ kusalañca nasukhasahagataṁ kusalañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nasukhasahagato nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

194\. Sukhasahagataṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasukhasahagato naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Nasukhasahagataṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasukhasahagato naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Sukhasahagataṁ akusalañca nasukhasahagataṁ akusalañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nasukhasahagato naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi. (Abyākatamūlaṁ tīṇiyeva paccayavasena.)
