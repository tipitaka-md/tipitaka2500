# 16 Hetuduka, Maggārammaṇattika

* Hetuduka, Maggārammaṇattika

(24.)

68\. Hetuṁ maggārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu namaggārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . (Sabbattha tīṇi.)

69\. Hetuṁ maggahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu namaggahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . (Sabbattha tīṇi.)

70\. Hetuṁ maggādhipatiṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu namaggādhipati dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
