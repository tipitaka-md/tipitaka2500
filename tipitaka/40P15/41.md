# 41 Cittasampayuttaduka, Kusalattika

* Cittasampayuttaduka, Kusalattika

(50.)

148\. Cittasampayuttaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nacittasampayutto nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.

149\. Cittasampayuttaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nacittasampayutto naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ. (Abyākatamūlaṁ tīṇiyeva paccayavasena.)
