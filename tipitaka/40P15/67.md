# 67 Niyyānikaduka, Kusalattika

* Niyyānikaduka, Kusalattika

(76.)

205\. Niyyānikaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naniyyāniko nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Aniyyānikaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naniyyāniko nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā dve…pe… avigate dve.

206\. Aniyyānikaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naniyyāniko naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ. (Abyākatamūlaṁ dve paccayavasena.)
