# 24 Hetusampayuttaduka, Kusalattika

* Hetusampayuttaduka, Kusalattika

(31.)

86\. Hetusampayuttaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetusampayutto nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.

87\. Hetusampayuttaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetusampayutto naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate tīṇi.

88\. Hetuvippayuttaṁ abyākataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā nahetuvippayutto naabyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ, ārammaṇe tīṇi, adhipatiyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate tīṇi.
