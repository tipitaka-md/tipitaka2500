# 42 Cittasaṃsaṭṭhaduka, Kusalattika

* Cittasaṃsaṭṭhaduka, Kusalattika

(51.)

150\. Cittasaṁsaṭṭhaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nacittasaṁsaṭṭho nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.

151\. Cittasaṁsaṭṭhaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nacittasaṁsaṭṭho naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ. (Abyākatamūlaṁ tīṇiyeva paccayavasena.)
