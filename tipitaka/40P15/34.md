# 34 Āsavasampayuttaduka, Kusalattika

* Āsavasampayuttaduka, Kusalattika

(43.)

125\. Āsavavippayuttaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavasampayutto nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.

126\. Āsavasampayuttaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavasampayutto naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

127\. Āsavavippayuttaṁ abyākataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā naāsavasampayutto naabyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
