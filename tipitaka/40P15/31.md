# 31 Kenaciviññeyyaduka, Kusalattika

* Kenaciviññeyyaduka, Kusalattika

(40.)

115\. Kenaci viññeyyaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nakenaci viññeyyo nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā nava…pe… avigate nava.

116\. Kenaci viññeyyaṁ akusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nakenaci viññeyyo naakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā nava…pe… avigate nava.

117\. Kenaci viññeyyaṁ abyākataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā nakenaci viññeyyo naabyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā nava…pe… avigate nava.

118\. Cūḷantaradukaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
