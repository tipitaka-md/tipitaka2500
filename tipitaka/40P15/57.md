# 57 Savitakkaduka, Kusalattika

* Savitakkaduka, Kusalattika

(66.)

185\. Savitakkaṁ kusalaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasavitakko nakusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… . Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
