# 2.1.1.13 Niruddhanirujjhamānavāra

* Niddesa

* Suddhacittasāmañña

* Puggalavāra

* Niruddhanirujjhamānavāra

(82.)

366\. » Niruddhaṁ nirujjhamānanti? No.

367\. « Nirujjhamānaṁ niruddhanti? No.

368\. » Na niruddhaṁ na nirujjhamānanti?

369\. Bhaṅgakkhaṇe na niruddhaṁ, no ca na nirujjhamānaṁ. Uppādakkhaṇe anāgatañca cittaṁ na ceva niruddhaṁ na ca nirujjhamānaṁ.

370\. « Na nirujjhamānaṁ na niruddhanti?

371\. Atītaṁ cittaṁ na nirujjhamānaṁ, no ca na niruddhaṁ. Uppādakkhaṇe anāgatañca cittaṁ na ceva nirujjhamānaṁ na ca niruddhaṁ.
