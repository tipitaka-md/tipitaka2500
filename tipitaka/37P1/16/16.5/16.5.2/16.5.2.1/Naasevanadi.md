2333\. Micchattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho micchattaniyato dhammo uppajjati nakammapaccayā, navipākapaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

2334\. Aniyataṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho aniyato dhammo uppajjati najhānapaccayā— pañcaviññāṇaṁ…pe… namaggapaccayā— ahetukaṁ aniyataṁ…pe… .
