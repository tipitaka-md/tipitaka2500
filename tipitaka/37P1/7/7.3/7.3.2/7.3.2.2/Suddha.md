(56.)

243\. Nahetuyā tettiṁsa, naārammaṇe satta, naadhipatiyā sattatiṁsa, naanantare nasamanantare naaññamaññe naupanissaye satta, napurejāte napacchājāte naāsevane sattatiṁsa, nakamme ekādasa, navipāke sattatiṁsa, naāhāre ekaṁ, naindriye ekaṁ, najhāne ekaṁ, namagge tettiṁsa, nasampayutte satta, navippayutte ekādasa, nonatthiyā satta, novigate satta.
