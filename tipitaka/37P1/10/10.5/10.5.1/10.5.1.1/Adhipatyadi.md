* Adhipatyādi

(67.)

1251\. Dassanena pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho dassanena pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— dassanena pahātabbahetukaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ saṁsaṭṭhā tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhā. (1)

1252\. Bhāvanāya pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho… ekaṁ.

1253\. Nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetukaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ saṁsaṭṭhā tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhā; anantarapaccayā… samanantarapaccayā.
