1257\. Dassanena pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati nahetupaccayā— vicikicchāsahagate khandhe saṁsaṭṭho vicikicchāsahagato moho. (1)
