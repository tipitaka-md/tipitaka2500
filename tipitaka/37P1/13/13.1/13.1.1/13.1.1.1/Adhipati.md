* Adhipati

(6.)

1884\. Parittaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca paritto dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— parittaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṁ, dve khandhe…pe… ekaṁ mahābhūtaṁ paṭicca tayo mahābhūtā…pe… mahābhūte paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ upādārūpaṁ. (1)

1885\. Mahaggataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca mahaggato dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— mahaggataṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhe…pe… . (1)

1886\. Mahaggataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca paritto dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— mahaggate khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (2)

1887\. Mahaggataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca paritto ca mahaggato ca dhammā uppajjanti adhipatipaccayā— mahaggataṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṁ…pe… dve khandhe…pe… . (3)

1888\. Appamāṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca appamāṇo dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— appamāṇaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhe…pe… . (1)

1889\. Appamāṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca paritto dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— appamāṇe khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (2)

1890\. Appamāṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca paritto ca appamāṇo ca dhammā uppajjanti adhipatipaccayā— appamāṇaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā cittasamuṭṭhānañca rūpaṁ…pe… dve khandhe…pe… . (3)

(7.)

1891\. Parittañca appamāṇañca dhammaṁ paṭicca paritto dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— appamāṇe khandhe ca mahābhūte ca paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (1)

1892\. Parittañca mahaggatañca dhammaṁ paṭicca paritto dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— mahaggate khandhe ca mahābhūte ca paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (1)
