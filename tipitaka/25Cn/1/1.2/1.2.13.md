# 1.2.13 Udayamāṇavapucchā

* Pārāyanavaggagāthā

* Pucchā

* Udayamāṇavapucchā

(130.)

144\. _“Jhāyiṁ virajamāsīnaṁ, (iccāyasmā udayo)_  
_Katakiccaṁ anāsavaṁ;_  
_Pāraguṁ sabbadhammānaṁ,_  
_Atthi pañhena āgamaṁ;_  
_Aññāvimokkhaṁ pabrūhi,_  
_Avijjāya pabhedanaṁ”. (1)_  

(131.)

145\. _“Pahānaṁ kāmacchandānaṁ, (udayāti bhagavā)_  
_Domanassāna cūbhayaṁ;_  
_Thinassa ca panūdanaṁ,_  
_Kukkuccānaṁ nivāraṇaṁ. (2)_  

(132.)

146\. _Upekkhāsatisaṁsuddhaṁ,_  
_dhammatakkapurejavaṁ;_  
_Aññāvimokkhaṁ pabrūmi,_  
_avijjāya pabhedanaṁ”. (3)_  

(133.)

147\. _“Kiṁsu saṁyojano loko,_  
_kiṁsu tassa vicāraṇaṁ;_  
_Kissassa vippahānena,_  
_nibbānaṁ iti vuccati”. (4)_  

(134.)

148\. _“Nandisaṁyojano loko,_  
_vitakkassa vicāraṇaṁ;_  
_Taṇhāya vippahānena,_  
_nibbānaṁ iti vuccati”. (5)_  

(135.)

149\. _“Kathaṁ satassa carato,_  
_viññāṇaṁ uparujjhati;_  
_Bhagavantaṁ puṭṭhumāgamma,_  
_taṁ suṇoma vaco tava”. (6)_  

(136.)

150\. _“Ajjhattañca bahiddhā ca,_  
_Vedanaṁ nābhinandato;_  
_Evaṁ satassa carato,_  
_Viññāṇaṁ uparujjhatī”ti. (7)_  

151\. Udayamāṇavapucchā terasamā.
