# 2.1.11 Abhabbasutta

* Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

* Khemavagga

* Abhabbasutta

(62.)

430\. “Nava, bhikkhave, dhamme appahāya abhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. Katame nava? Rāgaṁ, dosaṁ, mohaṁ, kodhaṁ, upanāhaṁ, makkhaṁ, paḷāsaṁ, issaṁ, macchariyaṁ— ime kho, bhikkhave, nava dhamme appahāya abhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ.

431\. Nava, bhikkhave, dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. Katame nava? Rāgaṁ, dosaṁ, mohaṁ, kodhaṁ, upanāhaṁ, makkhaṁ, paḷāsaṁ, issaṁ, macchariyaṁ— ime kho, bhikkhave, nava dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātun”ti.

432\. Ekādasamaṁ.

433\. Khemavaggo paṭhamo.

434\. 

435\. _Khemo ca amatañceva,_  
_Abhayaṁ passaddhiyena ca;_  
_Nirodho anupubbo ca,_  
_Dhammaṁ pahāya bhabbena cāti._
