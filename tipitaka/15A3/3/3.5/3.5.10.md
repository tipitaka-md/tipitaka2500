# 3.5.10 Pubbaṇhasutta

* Tatiyapaṇṇāsaka

* Maṅgalavagga

* Pubbaṇhasutta

(156.)

1089\. “Ye, bhikkhave, sattā pubbaṇhasamayaṁ kāyena sucaritaṁ caranti, vācāya sucaritaṁ caranti, manasā sucaritaṁ caranti, supubbaṇho, bhikkhave, tesaṁ sattānaṁ.

1090\. Ye, bhikkhave, sattā majjhanhikasamayaṁ kāyena sucaritaṁ caranti, vācāya sucaritaṁ caranti, manasā sucaritaṁ caranti, sumajjhanhiko, bhikkhave, tesaṁ sattānaṁ.

1091\. Ye, bhikkhave, sattā sāyanhasamayaṁ kāyena sucaritaṁ caranti, vācāya sucaritaṁ caranti, manasā sucaritaṁ caranti, susāyanho, bhikkhave, tesaṁ sattānanti.

1092\. _Sunakkhattaṁ sumaṅgalaṁ,_  
_suppabhātaṁ suhuṭṭhitaṁ;_  
_Sukhaṇo sumuhutto ca,_  
_suyiṭṭhaṁ brahmacārisu._  

1093\. _Padakkhiṇaṁ kāyakammaṁ,_  
_vācākammaṁ padakkhiṇaṁ;_  
_Padakkhiṇaṁ manokammaṁ,_  
_paṇīdhi te padakkhiṇe;_  
_Padakkhiṇāni katvāna,_  
_labhantatthe padakkhiṇe._  

1094\. _Te atthaladdhā sukhitā,_  
_viruḷhā buddhasāsane;_  
_Arogā sukhitā hotha,_  
_saha sabbehi ñātibhī”ti._  

1095\. Dasamaṁ.

1096\. Maṅgalavaggo pañcamo.

1097\. 

1098\. _Akusalañca sāvajjaṁ,_  
_visamāsucinā saha;_  
_Caturo khatā vandanā,_  
_pubbaṇhena ca te dasāti._  

1099\. Tatiyo paṇṇāsako samatto.
