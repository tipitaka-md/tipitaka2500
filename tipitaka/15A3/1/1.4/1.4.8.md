# 1.4.8 Dutiyacatumahārājasutta

* Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

* Devadūtavagga

* Dutiyacatumahārājasutta

(38.)

242\. “Bhūtapubbaṁ, bhikkhave, sakko devānamindo deve tāvatiṁse anunayamāno tāyaṁ velāyaṁ imaṁ gāthaṁ abhāsi—

243\. _‘Cātuddasiṁ pañcadasiṁ,_  
_yā ca pakkhassa aṭṭhamī;_  
_Pāṭihāriyapakkhañca,_  
_aṭṭhaṅgasusamāgataṁ;_  
_Uposathaṁ upavaseyya,_  
_yopissa mādiso naro’ti._  

244\. Sā kho panesā, bhikkhave, sakkena devānamindena gāthā duggītā na sugītā dubbhāsitā na subhāsitā. Taṁ kissa hetu? Sakko hi, bhikkhave, devānamindo aparimutto jātiyā jarāya maraṇena sokehi paridevehi dukkhehi domanassehi upāyāsehi, aparimutto dukkhasmāti vadāmi.

245\. Yo ca kho so, bhikkhave, bhikkhu arahaṁ khīṇāsavo vusitavā katakaraṇīyo ohitabhāro anuppattasadattho parikkhīṇabhavasaṁyojano sammadaññāvimutto, tassa kho etaṁ, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno kallaṁ vacanāya—

246\. _‘Cātuddasiṁ pañcadasiṁ,_  
_yā ca pakkhassa aṭṭhamī;_  
_Pāṭihāriyapakkhañca,_  
_aṭṭhaṅgasusamāgataṁ;_  
_Uposathaṁ upavaseyya,_  
_yopissa mādiso naro’ti._  

247\. Taṁ kissa hetu? So hi, bhikkhave, bhikkhu parimutto jātiyā jarāya maraṇena sokehi paridevehi dukkhehi domanassehi upāyāsehi, parimutto dukkhasmāti vadāmī”ti.

248\. Aṭṭhamaṁ.
