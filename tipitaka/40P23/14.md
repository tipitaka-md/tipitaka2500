# 14 Kusalattika, Micchattaniyatattika

* Kusalattika, Micchattaniyatattika

(18.)

63\. Nakusalaṁ namicchattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā akusalo micchattaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

64\. Nakusalaṁ nasammattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā kusalo sammattaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

65\. Nakusalaṁ naaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca abyākato aniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā pañca, adhipatiyā pañca…pe… avigate pañca.
