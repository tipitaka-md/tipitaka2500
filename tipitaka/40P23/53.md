# 53 Sanidassanattika, Micchattaniyatattika

* Sanidassanattika, Micchattaniyatattika

(62.)

195\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ namicchattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā anidassanaappaṭigho micchattaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

196\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nasammattaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā anidassanaappaṭigho sammattaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

197\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ naaniyataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sanidassanasappaṭigho aniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekavīsa, adhipatiyā ekavīsa…pe… avigate ekavīsa.
