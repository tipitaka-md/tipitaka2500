# 54 Sanidassanattika, Maggārammaṇattika

* Sanidassanattika, Maggārammaṇattika

(63.)

198\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ namaggārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā anidassanaappaṭigho maggārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

199\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ namaggahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā anidassanaappaṭigho maggahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

200\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ namaggādhipatiṁ dhammaṁ paccayā anidassanaappaṭigho maggādhipati dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
