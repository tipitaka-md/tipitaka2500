# 40 Sanidassanattika, Vedanāttika

* Sanidassanattika, Vedanāttika

(49.)

156\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nasukhāya vedanāya sampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho sukhāya vedanāya sampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

157\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nadukkhāya vedanāya sampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho dukkhāya vedanāya sampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

158\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ naadukkhamasukhāya vedanāya sampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho adukkhamasukhāya vedanāya sampayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
