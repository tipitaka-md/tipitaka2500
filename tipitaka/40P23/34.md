# 34 Maggārammaṇattika, Kusalattika

* Maggārammaṇattika, Kusalattika

(43.)

136\. Namaggārammaṇaṁ nakusalaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā maggārammaṇo kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

137\. Hetuyā pañcatiṁsa…pe… avigate pañcatiṁsa. (Naakusalaṁ naabyākataṁ natthi.)
