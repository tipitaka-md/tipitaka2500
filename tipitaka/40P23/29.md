# 29 Sekkhattika, Kusalattika

* Sekkhattika, Kusalattika

(38.)

121\. Nasekkhaṁ nakusalaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā sekkho kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha…pe… avigate cha.

122\. Nasekkhaṁ naakusalaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā nevasekkhanāsekkho akusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

123\. Nasekkhaṁ naabyākataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nevasekkhanāsekkho abyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā pañca, adhipatiyā pañca…pe… avigate pañca.
