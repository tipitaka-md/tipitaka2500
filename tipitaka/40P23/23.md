# 23 Saṃkiliṭṭhattika, Kusalattika

* Saṃkiliṭṭhattika, Kusalattika

(32.)

103\. Nasaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesikaṁ nakusalaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā asaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesiko kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha…pe… avigate cha.

104\. Nasaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesikaṁ naakusalaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā saṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesiko akusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

105\. Nasaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesikaṁ naabyākataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca asaṁkiliṭṭhasaṁkilesiko abyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha, adhipatiyā cha…pe… avigate cha.
