# 12 Kusalattika, Parittārammaṇattika

* Kusalattika, Parittārammaṇattika

(16.)

57\. Nakusalaṁ naparittārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca abyākato parittārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

58\. Nakusalaṁ namahaggatārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā kusalo mahaggatārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā nava…pe… vipāke tīṇi…pe… avigate nava.

59\. Nakusalaṁ naappamāṇārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā kusalo appamāṇārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha…pe… vipāke tīṇi…pe… avigate cha.
