# 45 Sanidassanattika, Pītittika

* Sanidassanattika, Pītittika

(54.)

171\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ napītisahagataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho pītisahagato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

172\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nasukhasahagataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho sukhasahagato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

173\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ naupekkhāsahagataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho upekkhāsahagato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
