# 47 Sanidassanattika, Dassanahetuttika

* Sanidassanattika, Dassanahetuttika

(56.)

177\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nadassanena pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho dassanena pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

178\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca anidassanaappaṭigho bhāvanāya pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

179\. Nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ nanevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sanidassanasappaṭigho nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbahetuko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekavīsa, ārammaṇe tīṇi…pe… avigate ekavīsa.
