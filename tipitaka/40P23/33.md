# 33 Micchattaniyatattika, Kusalattika

* Micchattaniyatattika, Kusalattika

(42.)

133\. Namicchattaniyataṁ nakusalaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā sammattaniyato kusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha…pe… avigate cha.

134\. Namicchattaniyataṁ naakusalaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā micchattaniyato akusalo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha…pe… avigate cha.

135\. Namicchattaniyataṁ naabyākataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca aniyato abyākato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha, adhipatiyā cha…pe… avigate cha.
