# 92 Sanidassanattika, Niyyānikaduka

* Sanidassanattika, Niyyānikaduka

(143.)

340\. Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ niyyānikaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassanaappaṭigho naniyyāniko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha, adhipatiyā cha…pe… avigate cha.

341\. Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ aniyyānikaṁ dhammaṁ paccayā nasanidassanasappaṭigho naaniyyāniko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
