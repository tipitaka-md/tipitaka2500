# 40 Maggārammaṇattika, Hetuduka

* Maggārammaṇattika, Hetuduka

(82.)

213\. Maggārammaṇaṁ hetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca namaggārammaṇo nahetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā pañcatiṁsa, ārammaṇe chabbīsa…pe… avigate pañcatiṁsa.

214\. Maggārammaṇaṁ nahetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca namaggahetuko nanahetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā chabbīsa…pe… avigate chabbīsa.
