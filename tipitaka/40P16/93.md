# 93 Sanidassanattika, Niyataduka

* Sanidassanattika, Niyataduka

(144.)

342\. Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ niyataṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassanaappaṭigho naniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā cha, adhipatiyā cha…pe… avigate cha.

343\. Anidassanaappaṭighaṁ aniyataṁ dhammaṁ paccayā nasanidassanasappaṭigho naaniyato dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.
