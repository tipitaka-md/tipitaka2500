# Hetu

* Hetusahetukaduka

* Paṭiccavāra

* Paccayānuloma

* Vibhaṅgavāra

* Hetu

(127.)

353\. Hetuñceva sahetukañca dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu ceva sahetuko ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— alobhaṁ paṭicca adoso amoho (cakkaṁ). Lobhaṁ paṭicca moho (cakkaṁ); paṭisandhikkhaṇe alobhaṁ paṭicca adoso amoho. (Cakkaṁ.) (1)

354\. Hetuñceva sahetukañca dhammaṁ paṭicca sahetuko ceva na ca hetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— hetuṁ paṭicca sampayuttakā khandhā; paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (2)

355\. Hetuñceva sahetukañca dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu ceva sahetuko ca sahetuko ceva na ca hetu ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā— alobhaṁ paṭicca adoso amoho sampayuttakā ca khandhā (cakkaṁ). Lobhaṁ paṭicca moho sampayuttakā ca khandhā (cakkaṁ); paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (3)

(128.)

356\. Sahetukañceva na ca hetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sahetuko ceva na ca hetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— sahetukañceva na ca hetuṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhe…pe… paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (1)

357\. Sahetukañceva na ca hetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu ceva sahetuko ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— sahetuke ceva na ca hetū khandhe paṭicca hetū; paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (2)

358\. Sahetukañceva na ca hetuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu ceva sahetuko ca sahetuko ceva na ca hetu ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā— sahetukañceva na ca hetuṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ paṭicca tayo khandhā hetu ca…pe… dve khandhe…pe… paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (3)

(129.)

359\. Hetuñceva sahetukañca sahetukañceva na ca hetuñca dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu ceva sahetuko ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— alobhañca sampayuttake ca khandhe paṭicca adoso amoho (cakkaṁ). Lobhañca sampayuttake ca khandhe paṭicca moho (cakkaṁ); paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (1)

360\. Hetuñceva sahetukañca sahetukañceva na ca hetuñca dhammaṁ paṭicca sahetuko ceva na ca hetu dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— sahetukañceva na ca hetuṁ ekaṁ khandhañca hetuñca paṭicca tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhe…pe… paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (2)

361\. Hetuñceva sahetukañca sahetukañceva na ca hetuñca dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu ceva sahetuko ca sahetuko ceva na ca hetu ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā— sahetukañceva na ca hetuṁ ekaṁ khandhañca alobhañca paṭicca tayo khandhā adoso amoho ca…pe… dve khandhe…pe… paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (3)

362\. (Saṁkhittaṁ. Evaṁ vitthāretabbaṁ.)
