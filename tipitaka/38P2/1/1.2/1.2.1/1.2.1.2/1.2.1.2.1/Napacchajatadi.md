* Hetugocchaka

* Sahetukaduka

* Paṭiccavāra

* Paccayapaccanīya

* Vibhaṅgavāra

* Napacchājātādi

(66.)

179\. Sahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sahetuko dhammo uppajjati napacchājātapaccayā… naāsevanapaccayā… nakammapaccayā— sahetuke khandhe paṭicca sahetukā cetanā. (1)

180\. Ahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca ahetuko dhammo uppajjati nakammapaccayā— ahetuke khandhe paṭicca ahetukā cetanā… bāhiraṁ… āhārasamuṭṭhānaṁ… utusamuṭṭhānaṁ…pe… . (1)

181\. Ahetukaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sahetuko dhammo uppajjati nakammapaccayā— vicikicchāsahagataṁ uddhaccasahagataṁ mohaṁ paṭicca sampayuttakā cetanā. (2)

182\. Sahetukañca ahetukañca dhammaṁ paṭicca sahetuko dhammo uppajjati nakammapaccayā— vicikicchāsahagate uddhaccasahagate khandhe ca mohañca paṭicca sampayuttakā cetanā… navipākapaccayā. (Paṭisandhi natthi.)
