# Paccanīyuddhāra

* Kilesagocchaka

* Saṃkiliṭṭhaduka

* Pañhāvāra

* Paccanīyuddhāra

(68.)

3989\. Saṁkiliṭṭho dhammo saṁkiliṭṭhassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… sahajātapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo. (1)

3990\. Saṁkiliṭṭho dhammo asaṁkiliṭṭhassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… sahajātapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo… pacchājātapaccayena paccayo… kammapaccayena paccayo. (2)

3991\. Saṁkiliṭṭho dhammo saṁkiliṭṭhassa ca asaṁkiliṭṭhassa ca dhammassa sahajātapaccayena paccayo. (3)

(69.)

3992\. Asaṁkiliṭṭho dhammo asaṁkiliṭṭhassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… sahajātapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo… purejātapaccayena paccayo… pacchājātapaccayena paccayo… kammapaccayena paccayo… āhārapaccayena paccayo… indriyapaccayena paccayo. (1)

3993\. Asaṁkiliṭṭho dhammo saṁkiliṭṭhassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo… purejātapaccayena paccayo. (2)

3994\. Saṁkiliṭṭho ca asaṁkiliṭṭho ca dhammā saṁkiliṭṭhassa dhammassa sahajātaṁ… purejātaṁ. (1)

3995\. Saṁkiliṭṭho ca asaṁkiliṭṭho ca dhammā asaṁkiliṭṭhassa dhammassa sahajātaṁ… pacchājātaṁ… āhāraṁ… indriyaṁ. (2)
