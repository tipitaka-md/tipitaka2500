(45.)

1434\. Saññojanasampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca saññojanasampayutto dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā… tīṇi.

1435\. Saññojanavippayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca saññojanavippayutto dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā… ekaṁ.

1436\. Saññojanasampayuttañca saññojanavippayuttañca dhammaṁ paṭicca saññojanavippayutto dhammo uppajjati adhipatipaccayā— saññojanasampayuttake khandhe ca mahābhūte ca paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (1) (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
