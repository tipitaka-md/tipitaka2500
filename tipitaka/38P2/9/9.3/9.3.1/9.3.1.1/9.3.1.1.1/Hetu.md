2214\. Parāmāsasampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca parāmāsavippayutto dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā— parāmāsasampayutte khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (2)
