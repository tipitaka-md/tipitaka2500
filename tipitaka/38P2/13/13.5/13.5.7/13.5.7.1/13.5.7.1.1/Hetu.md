(116.)

4324\. Savitakko dhammo savitakkassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo— savitakkā hetū sampayuttakānaṁ khandhānaṁ hetupaccayena paccayo; paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . Savitakko dhammo avitakkassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo— savitakkā hetū vitakkassa cittasamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṁ hetupaccayena paccayo; paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… . (Mūlaṁ kātabbaṁ.) Savitakkā hetū sampayuttakānaṁ khandhānaṁ vitakkassa ca cittasamuṭṭhānānañca rūpānaṁ hetupaccayena paccayo; paṭisandhikkhaṇe…pe… (3)
