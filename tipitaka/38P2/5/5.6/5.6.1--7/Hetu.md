1883\. (Yathā cūḷantaraduke lokiyadukaṁ, evaṁ vitthāretabbaṁ ninnānākaraṇaṁ.)

1884\. Ganthavippayuttaganthaniyadukaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.

1885\. Ganthagocchakaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
