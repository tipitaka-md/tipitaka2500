# 7.7 Samādāyapannarasakādi

* Kathinakkhandhaka

* Samādāyapannarasakādi

(316.)

1446\. Bhikkhu atthatakathino cīvaraṁ samādāya pakkamati…pe… .

1447\. (Ādāyavārasadisaṁ evaṁ vitthāretabbaṁ.)

1448\. Bhikkhu atthatakathino vippakatacīvaraṁ ādāya pakkamati. Tassa bahisīmagatassa evaṁ hoti— “idhevimaṁ cīvaraṁ kāressaṁ, na paccessan”ti. So taṁ cīvaraṁ kāreti. Tassa bhikkhuno niṭṭhānantiko kathinuddhāro…pe… .

1449\. (Samādāyavārasadisaṁ evaṁ vitthāretabbaṁ.)
