# 3.15 Āyusaṅkhāraossajjana

* Mahāparinibbānasutta

* Āyusaṅkhāraossajjana

(169.)

362\. Atha kho bhagavā cāpāle cetiye sato sampajāno āyusaṅkhāraṁ ossaji. Ossaṭṭhe ca bhagavatā āyusaṅkhāre mahābhūmicālo ahosi bhiṁsanako salomahaṁso, devadundubhiyo ca phaliṁsu. Atha kho bhagavā etamatthaṁ viditvā tāyaṁ velāyaṁ imaṁ udānaṁ udānesi—

363\. _“Tulamatulañca sambhavaṁ,_  
_Bhavasaṅkhāramavassaji muni;_  
_Ajjhattarato samāhito,_  
_Abhindi kavacamivattasambhavan”ti._
