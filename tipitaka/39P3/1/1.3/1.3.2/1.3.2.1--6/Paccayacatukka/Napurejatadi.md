(125.)

218\. Hetuṁ vipākadhammadhammaṁ paṭicca hetu vipākadhammadhammo uppajjati napurejātapaccayā… nava… napacchājātapaccayā… nava… naāsevanapaccayā… nava.

(126.)

219\. Hetuṁ vipākadhammadhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu vipākadhammadhammo uppajjati nakammapaccayā. (1)

220\. Nahetuṁ vipākadhammadhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu vipākadhammadhammo uppajjati nakammapaccayā. (1)

221\. Hetuṁ vipākadhammadhammañca nahetuṁ vipākadhammadhammañca paṭicca nahetu vipākadhammadhammo uppajjati nakammapaccayā. (1) (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

(127.)

222\. Nahetuyā ekaṁ, naadhipatiyā nava, napurejāte nava, napacchājāte nava, naāsevane nava, nakamme tīṇi, navipāke nava, navippayutte nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ, paccanīyaṁ.)

223\. Hetupaccayā naadhipatiyā nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ, anulomapaccanīyaṁ.)

224\. Nahetupaccayā ārammaṇe ekaṁ. (Saṁkhittaṁ, paccanīyānulomaṁ.)
