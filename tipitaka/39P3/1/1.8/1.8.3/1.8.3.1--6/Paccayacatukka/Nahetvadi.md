# Nahetvādi

* Hetuduka, Tika

* Hetuduka, Dassanenapahātabbattika

* Nevadassanenanabhāvanāyapahātabbapada

* Paṭiccādivāra

* Paccayacatukka

* Nahetvādi

(276.)

557\. Nahetuṁ nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbo dhammo uppajjati nahetupaccayā.

558\. Hetuṁ nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbo dhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā. (1)

559\. Nahetuṁ nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbo dhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā. (1)

560\. Hetuṁ nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbañca nahetuṁ nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbo dhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā. (1)

561\. Hetuṁ nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu nevadassanena nabhāvanāya pahātabbo dhammo uppajjati naadhipatipaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

(277.)

562\. Nahetuyā ekaṁ, naārammaṇe tīṇi, naadhipatiyā nava, naanantare tīṇi, nasamanantare tīṇi, naaññamaññe tīṇi, naupanissaye tīṇi, napurejāte nava, napacchājāte nava, naāsevane nava, nakamme tīṇi, navipāke nava, naāhāre ekaṁ, naindriye ekaṁ, najhāne ekaṁ, namagge ekaṁ, nasampayutte tīṇi, navippayutte nava, nonatthiyā tīṇi, novigate tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

563\. Hetupaccayā naārammaṇe tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

564\. Nahetupaccayā ārammaṇe ekaṁ. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

565\. (Sahajātavāropi paccayavāropi nissayavāropi saṁsaṭṭhavāropi sampayuttavāropi paṭiccavārasadisā vitthāretabbā.)
