* Paccanīyuddhāra

(531.)

1193\. Hetu ajjhattārammaṇo dhammo hetussa ajjhattārammaṇassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… sahajātapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

(532.)

1194\. Nahetuyā nava, naārammaṇe nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

1195\. Hetupaccayā naārammaṇe tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

1196\. Nahetupaccayā ārammaṇe nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

1197\. (Yathā kusalattike pañhāvārassa anulomampi paccanīyampi anulomapaccanīyampi paccanīyānulomampi gaṇitaṁ, evaṁ gaṇetabbaṁ.)
