* Paccayacatukka

* Hetu-ārammaṇa

(206.)

386\. Hetu asaṁkiliṭṭhaasaṁkilesiko dhammo hetussa asaṁkiliṭṭhaasaṁkilesikassa dhammassa hetupaccayena paccayo… tīṇi.

387\. Nahetu asaṁkiliṭṭhaasaṁkilesiko dhammo nahetussa asaṁkiliṭṭhaasaṁkilesikassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

(207.)

388\. Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe tīṇi, adhipatiyā cha, anantare nava, samanantare nava, sahajāte nava, aññamaññe nava, nissaye nava, upanissaye nava, kamme tīṇi, vipāke nava, āhāre tīṇi, indriye nava, jhāne tīṇi, magge nava, sampayutte nava, atthiyā nava, natthiyā nava, vigate nava, avigate nava. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)
