* Pañhāvāra

* Paccanīyuddhāra

(45.)

1356\. Hetusampayutto abyākato dhammo hetusampayuttassa abyākatassa dhammassa ārammaṇapaccayena paccayo… sahajātapaccayena paccayo… upanissayapaccayena paccayo.

(46.)

1357\. Nahetuyā satta, naārammaṇe satta. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

1358\. Hetupaccayā naārammaṇe tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

1359\. Nahetupaccayā ārammaṇe cattāri. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

1360\. (Yathā kusalattike pañhāvārassa anulomampi paccanīyampi anulomapaccanīyampi paccanīyānulomampi gaṇitaṁ, evaṁ gaṇetabbaṁ.)

1361\. Hetusampayuttadukakusalattikaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.
