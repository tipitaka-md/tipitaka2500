# 1.4.4.4 Adhiṭṭhānaṭṭhaniddesa

* Mahāvagga

* Indriyakathā

* Catutthasuttantaniddesa

* Adhiṭṭhānaṭṭhaniddesa

(202.)

1083\. Kathaṁ adhiṭṭhānaṭṭhena indriyāni daṭṭhabbāni? Saddhindriyassa bhāvanāya chando uppajjati— chandavasena saddhāvasena saddhindriyaṁ adhiṭṭhāti. Chandavasena pāmojjaṁ uppajjati— pāmojjavasena saddhāvasena saddhindriyaṁ adhiṭṭhāti…pe… evaṁ adhiṭṭhānaṭṭhena indriyāni daṭṭhabbāni.
