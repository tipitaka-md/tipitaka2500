# 8.1.4 Nicchavisutta

* Lakkhaṇasaṃyutta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Nicchavisutta

(205.)

1167\. “Idhāhaṁ, āvuso, gijjhakūṭā pabbatā orohanto addasaṁ nicchaviṁ purisaṁ vehāsaṁ gacchantaṁ. Tamenaṁ gijjhāpi kākāpi kulalāpi anupatitvā anupatitvā vitacchenti virājenti. So sudaṁ aṭṭassaraṁ karoti…pe… eso, bhikkhave, satto imasmiṁyeva rājagahe orabbhiko ahosi…pe… .

1168\. Catutthaṁ.
