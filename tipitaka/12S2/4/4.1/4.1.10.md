# 4.1.10 Puggalasutta

* Anamataggasaṃyutta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Puggalasutta

(133.)

853\. Ekaṁ samayaṁ bhagavā rājagahe viharati gijjhakūṭe pabbate. Tatra kho bhagavā bhikkhū āmantesi— “bhikkhavo”ti. “Bhadante”ti te bhikkhū bhagavato paccassosuṁ. Bhagavā etadavoca—

854\. “Anamataggoyaṁ, bhikkhave, saṁsāro…pe… ekapuggalassa, bhikkhave, kappaṁ sandhāvato saṁsarato siyā evaṁ mahā aṭṭhikaṅkalo aṭṭhipuñjo aṭṭhirāsi yathāyaṁ vepullo pabbato, sace saṁhārako assa, sambhatañca na vinasseyya. Taṁ kissa hetu? Anamataggoyaṁ, bhikkhave, saṁsāro…pe… alaṁ vimuccitun”ti.

855\. Idamavoca bhagavā. Idaṁ vatvāna sugato athāparaṁ etadavoca satthā—

856\. _“Ekassekena kappena,_  
_puggalassaṭṭhisañcayo;_  
_Siyā pabbatasamo rāsi,_  
_iti vuttaṁ mahesinā._  

857\. _So kho panāyaṁ akkhāto,_  
_vepullo pabbato mahā;_  
_Uttaro gijjhakūṭassa,_  
_magadhānaṁ giribbaje._  

858\. _Yato ca ariyasaccāni,_  
_sammappaññāya passati;_  
_Dukkhaṁ dukkhasamuppādaṁ,_  
_dukkhassa ca atikkamaṁ;_  
_Ariyaṁ caṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ,_  
_dukkhūpasamagāminaṁ._  

859\. _Sa sattakkhattuṁparamaṁ,_  
_sandhāvitvāna puggalo;_  
_Dukkhassantakaro hoti,_  
_sabbasaṁyojanakkhayā”ti._  

860\. Dasamaṁ.

861\. Paṭhamo vaggo.

862\. 

863\. _Tiṇakaṭṭhañca pathavī,_  
_assu khīrañca pabbataṁ;_  
_Sāsapā sāvakā gaṅgā,_  
_daṇḍo ca puggalena cāti._
