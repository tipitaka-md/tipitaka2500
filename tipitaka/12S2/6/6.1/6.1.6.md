# 6.1.6 Asanisutta

* Lābhasakkārasaṃyutta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Asanisutta

(162.)

1033\. Sāvatthiyaṁ viharati. “Dāruṇo, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… adhigamāya. Kaṁ, bhikkhave, asanivicakkaṁ āgacchatu, sekhaṁ appattamānasaṁ lābhasakkārasiloko anupāpuṇātu.

1034\. Asanivicakkanti kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasilokassetaṁ adhivacanaṁ. Evaṁ dāruṇo kho, bhikkhave, lābhasakkārasiloko…pe… evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti.

1035\. Chaṭṭhaṁ.
