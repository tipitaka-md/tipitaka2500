# 4.13 Akkharukkhapetavatthu

* Mahāvagga

* Akkharukkhapetavatthu

(800.)

860\. _“Yaṁ dadāti na taṁ hoti,_  
_Detheva dānaṁ datvā ubhayaṁ tarati;_  
_Ubhayaṁ tena dānena gacchati,_  
_Jāgaratha māpamajjathā”ti._  

861\. Akkharukkhapetavatthu terasamaṁ.
