# 1.1.5 Mahāgovindacariya

* Akittivagga

* Dānapāramī

* Mahāgovindacariya

(37.)

42\. _“Punāparaṁ yadā homi,_  
_sattarājapurohito;_  
_Pūjito naradevehi,_  
_mahāgovindabrāhmaṇo._  

(38.)

43\. _Tadāhaṁ sattarajjesu,_  
_Yaṁ me āsi upāyanaṁ;_  
_Tena demi mahādānaṁ,_  
_Akkhobbhaṁ sāgarūpamaṁ._  

(39.)

44\. _Na me dessaṁ dhanaṁ dhaññaṁ,_  
_Napi natthi nicayo mayi;_  
_Sabbaññutaṁ piyaṁ mayhaṁ,_  
_Tasmā demi varaṁ dhanan”ti._  

45\. Mahāgovindacariyaṁ pañcamaṁ.
