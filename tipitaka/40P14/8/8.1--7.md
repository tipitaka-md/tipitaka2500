# 8.1--7 Paṭiccādivāra

* Sanidassanaduka

* Paṭiccādivāra

(11.)

35\. Anidassanaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naanidassano dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Anidassanaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassano dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Anidassanaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasanidassano ca naanidassano ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (3) (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

36\. Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe ekaṁ…pe… avigate tīṇi. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)
