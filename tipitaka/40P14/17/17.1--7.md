# 17.1--7 Paṭiccādivāra

* Āsavaāsavasampayuttaduka

* Paṭiccādivāra

(20.)

68\. Āsavañceva āsavasampayuttañca dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavo ceva naāsavavippayutto ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

69\. Āsavasampayuttañceva no ca āsavaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naāsavavippayutto ceva nano ca āsavo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

70\. Āsavañceva āsavasampayuttañca āsavavippayuttañceva no ca āsavañca dhammaṁ paṭicca noāsavo ceva naāsavavippayutto ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

71\. Hetuyā nava. (Sabbattha nava.)
