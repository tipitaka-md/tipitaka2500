# 2.2.6 Adhikaraṇavāra

* Bhikkhunivibhaṅga

* Dutiyabhāga

* Adhikaraṇavāra

(254.)

1064\. Kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ katamaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ? Kāyasaṁsaggaṁ sādiyanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ, āpattādhikaraṇaṁ…pe… . (1)

1065\. Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ katamaṁ adhikaraṇaṁ? Dadhiṁ viññāpetvā bhuñjanapaccayā āpattiyo catunnaṁ adhikaraṇānaṁ— āpattādhikaraṇaṁ. (2)

1066\. Adhikaraṇavāro niṭṭhito chaṭṭho.
