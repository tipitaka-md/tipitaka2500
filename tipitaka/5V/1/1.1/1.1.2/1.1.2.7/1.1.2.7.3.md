# 1.1.2.7.3 Khambhakatavagga

* Bhikkhuvibhaṅga

* Paṭhamabhāga

* Katāpattivāra

* Sekhiyakaṇḍa

* Khambhakatavagga

(177.)

513\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca khambhakato antaraghare gacchanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (1)

514\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca khambhakato antaraghare nisīdanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (2)

515\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca oguṇṭhito antaraghare gacchanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (3)

516\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca oguṇṭhito antaraghare nisīdanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (4)

517\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca ukkuṭikāya antaraghare gacchanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (5)

518\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca pallatthikāya antaraghare nisīdanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (6)

519\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca asakkaccaṁ piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggaṇhanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (7)

520\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca tahaṁ tahaṁ olokento piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggaṇhanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (8)

521\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca sūpaññeva bahuṁ paṭiggaṇhanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (9)

522\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca thūpīkataṁ piṇḍapātaṁ paṭiggaṇhanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (10)

523\. Khambhakatavaggo tatiyo.
