# 1.1.2.7.6 Surusuruvagga

* Bhikkhuvibhaṅga

* Paṭhamabhāga

* Katāpattivāra

* Sekhiyakaṇḍa

* Surusuruvagga

(180.)

546\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca surusurukārakaṁ bhuñjanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (1)

547\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca hatthanillehakaṁ bhuñjanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (2)

548\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca pattanillehakaṁ bhuñjanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (3)

549\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca oṭṭhanillehakaṁ bhuñjanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (4)

550\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca sāmisena hatthena pānīyathālakaṁ paṭiggaṇhanto ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (5)

551\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca sasitthakaṁ pattadhovanaṁ antaraghare chaḍḍento ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (6)

552\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca chattapāṇissa dhammaṁ desento ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (7)

553\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca daṇḍapāṇissa dhammaṁ desento ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (8)

554\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca satthapāṇissa dhammaṁ desento ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (9)

555\. Anādariyaṁ paṭicca āvudhapāṇissa dhammaṁ desento ekaṁ āpattiṁ āpajjati. Dukkaṭaṁ. (10)

556\. Surusuruvaggo chaṭṭho.
