# 13.2 Codakādipaṭipatti

* Codanākaṇḍa

* Codakādipaṭipatti

(363.)

1798\. Codakena kathaṁ paṭipajjitabbaṁ? Cuditakena kathaṁ paṭipajjitabbaṁ? Saṁghena kathaṁ paṭipajjitabbaṁ? Anuvijjakena kathaṁ paṭipajjitabbaṁ? Codakena kathaṁ paṭipajjitabbanti? Codakena pañcasu dhammesu patiṭṭhāya paro codetabbo. Kālena vakkhāmi no akālena, bhūtena vakkhāmi no abhūtena, saṇhena vakkhāmi no pharusena, atthasaṁhitena vakkhāmi no anatthasaṁhitena, mettācitto vakkhāmi no dosantaroti— codakena evaṁ paṭipajjitabbaṁ. Cuditakena kathaṁ paṭipajjitabbanti? Cuditakena dvīsu dhammesu paṭipajjitabbaṁ. Sacce ca akuppe ca— cuditakena evaṁ paṭipajjitabbaṁ. Saṁghena kathaṁ paṭipajjitabbanti? Saṁghena otiṇṇānotiṇṇaṁ jānitabbaṁ. Saṁghena evaṁ paṭipajjitabbaṁ. Anuvijjakena kathaṁ paṭipajjitabbanti? Anuvijjakena yena dhammena yena vinayena yena satthusāsanena taṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasammati tathā taṁ adhikaraṇaṁ vūpasametabbaṁ. Anuvijjakena evaṁ paṭipajjitabbaṁ.

(364.)

1799\. _Uposatho kimatthāya,_  
_Pavāraṇā kissa kāraṇā;_  
_Parivāso kimatthāya,_  
_Mūlāyapaṭikassanā kissa kāraṇā;_  
_Mānattaṁ kimatthāya,_  
_Abbhānaṁ kissa kāraṇā._  

1800\. _Uposatho sāmaggatthāya,_  
_Visuddhatthāya pavāraṇā;_  
_Parivāso mānattatthāya,_  
_Mūlāyapaṭikassanā niggahatthāya;_  
_Mānattaṁ abbhānatthāya,_  
_Visuddhatthāya abbhānaṁ._  

1801\. _Chandā dosā bhayā mohā,_  
_there ca paribhāsati;_  
_Kāyassa bhedā duppañño,_  
_khato upahatindriyo;_  
_Nirayaṁ gacchati dummedho,_  
_na ca sikkhāya gāravo._  

1802\. _Na ca āmisaṁ nissāya,_  
_na ca nissāya puggalaṁ;_  
_Ubho ete vivajjetvā,_  
_yathādhammo tathā kare._
