# 1.3.5 Brahmacariyasutta

* Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

* Uruvelavagga

* Brahmacariyasutta

(25.)

176\. “Nayidaṁ, bhikkhave, brahmacariyaṁ vussati janakuhanatthaṁ, na janalapanatthaṁ, na lābhasakkārasilokānisaṁsatthaṁ, na itivādappamokkhānisaṁsatthaṁ, na ‘iti maṁ jano jānātū’ti. Atha kho idaṁ, bhikkhave, brahmacariyaṁ vussati saṁvaratthaṁ pahānatthaṁ virāgatthaṁ nirodhatthanti.

177\. _Saṁvaratthaṁ pahānatthaṁ,_  
_brahmacariyaṁ anītihaṁ;_  
_Adesayi so bhagavā,_  
_nibbānogadhagāminaṁ;_  
_Esa maggo mahantehi,_  
_anuyāto mahesibhi._  

178\. _Ye ca taṁ paṭipajjanti,_  
_yathā buddhena desitaṁ;_  
_Dukkhassantaṁ karissanti,_  
_satthusāsanakārino”ti._  

179\. Pañcamaṁ.
