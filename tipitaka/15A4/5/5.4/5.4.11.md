# 5.4.11 Sappurisānisaṃsasutta

* Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

* Kammavagga

* Sappurisānisaṃsasutta

(242.)

1476\. “Sappurisaṁ, bhikkhave, nissāya cattāro ānisaṁsā pāṭikaṅkhā. Katame cattāro? Ariyena sīlena vaḍḍhati, ariyena samādhinā vaḍḍhati, ariyāya paññāya vaḍḍhati, ariyāya vimuttiyā vaḍḍhati— sappurisaṁ, bhikkhave, nissāya ime cattāro ānisaṁsā pāṭikaṅkhā”ti.

1477\. Ekādasamaṁ.

1478\. Kammavaggo catuttho.

1479\. 

1480\. _Saṁkhitta vitthāra soṇakāyana,_  
_Sikkhāpadaṁ ariyamaggo bojjhaṅgaṁ;_  
_Sāvajjañceva abyābajjhaṁ,_  
_Samaṇo ca sappurisānisaṁsoti._
