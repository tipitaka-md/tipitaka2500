# 5.4.1--7 Paṭiccādivāra

* Kusalattika, Mahantaradukaupādānagocchaka

* Kusalattika, Cittasampayuttadukādi

* Paṭiccādivāra

(17.)

1382\. Kusalaṁ cittasampayuttaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca…pe… kusalaṁ cittasaṁsaṭṭhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca…pe… kusalaṁ cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca…pe… kusalaṁ cittasahabhuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca…pe… kusalaṁ cittānuparivattiṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca…pe… kusalaṁ cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca…pe… kusalaṁ cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānasahabhuṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca…pe… kusalaṁ cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivattiṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca kusalo cittasaṁsaṭṭhasamuṭṭhānānuparivatti dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

1383\. Hetuyā tīṇi, ārammaṇe tīṇi…pe… vipāke ekaṁ…pe… avigate tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

1384\. (Sahajātavārampi…pe… sampayuttavārampi pañhāvārampi evaṁ vitthāretabbaṁ.)
