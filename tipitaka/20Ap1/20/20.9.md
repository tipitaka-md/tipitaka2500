# 20.9 Kiṃkaṇikapupphiyattheraapadāna

* Tamālapupphiyavagga

* Kiṃkaṇikapupphiyattheraapadāna

(47.)

2728\. _“Sumaṅgaloti nāmena,_  
_sayambhū aparājito;_  
_Pavanā nikkhamitvāna,_  
_nagaraṁ pāvisī jino._  

(48.)

2729\. _Piṇḍacāraṁ caritvāna,_  
_nikkhamma nagarā muni;_  
_Katakiccova sambuddho,_  
_so vasī vanamantare._  

(49.)

2730\. _Kiṁkaṇipupphaṁ paggayha,_  
_buddhassa abhiropayiṁ;_  
_Pasannacitto sumano,_  
_sayambhussa mahesino._  

(50.)

2731\. _Catunnavutito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(51.)

2732\. _Chaḷāsītimhito kappe,_  
_apilāsisanāmako;_  
_Sattaratanasampanno,_  
_cakkavattī mahabbalo._  

(52.)

2733\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

2734\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā kiṁkaṇikapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2735\. Kiṁkaṇikapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ navamaṁ.
