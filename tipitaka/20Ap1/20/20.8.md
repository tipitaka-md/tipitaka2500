# 20.8 Muṭṭhipūjakattheraapadāna

* Tamālapupphiyavagga

* Muṭṭhipūjakattheraapadāna

(42.)

2721\. _“Sumedho nāma bhagavā,_  
_lokajeṭṭho narāsabho;_  
_Pacchime anukampāya,_  
_padhānaṁ padahī jino._  

(43.)

2722\. _Tassa caṅkamamānassa,_  
_dvipadindassa tādino;_  
_Girinelassa pupphānaṁ,_  
_muṭṭhiṁ buddhassa ropayiṁ._  

(44.)

2723\. _Tena cittappasādena,_  
_sukkamūlena codito;_  
_Tiṁsakappasahassāni,_  
_duggatiṁ nupapajjahaṁ._  

(45.)

2724\. _Tevīsatikappasate,_  
_sunelo nāma khattiyo;_  
_Sattaratanasampanno,_  
_eko āsiṁ mahabbalo._  

(46.)

2725\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

2726\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā muṭṭhipūjako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2727\. Muṭṭhipūjakattherassāpadānaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
