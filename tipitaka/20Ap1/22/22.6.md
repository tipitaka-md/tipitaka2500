# 22.6 Sandhitattheraapadāna

* Hatthivagga

* Sandhitattheraapadāna

(27.)

2858\. _“Assatthe haritobhāse,_  
_saṁvirūḷhamhi pādape;_  
_Ekaṁ buddhagataṁ saññaṁ,_  
_alabhiṁhaṁ patissato._  

(28.)

2859\. _Ekattiṁse ito kappe,_  
_yaṁ saññamalabhiṁ tadā;_  
_Tassā saññāya vāhasā,_  
_patto me āsavakkhayo._  

(29.)

2860\. _Ito terasakappamhi,_  
_dhaniṭṭho nāma khattiyo;_  
_Sattaratanasampanno,_  
_cakkavattī mahabbalo._  

(30.)

2861\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

2862\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā sandhito thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2863\. Sandhitattherassāpadānaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
