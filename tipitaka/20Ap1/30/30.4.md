# 30.4 Saddasaññakattheraapadāna

* Citakapūjakavagga

* Saddasaññakattheraapadāna

(17.)

3380\. _“Anuggatamhi ādicce,_  
_panādo vipulo ahu;_  
_Buddhaseṭṭhassa lokamhi,_  
_pātubhāvo mahesino._  

(18.)

3381\. _Ghosamassosahaṁ tattha,_  
_na ca passāmi taṁ jinaṁ;_  
_Maraṇañca anuppatto,_  
_buddhasaññamanussariṁ._  

(19.)

3382\. _Catunnavutito kappe,_  
_yaṁ saññamalabhiṁ tadā;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(20.)

3383\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

3384\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā saddasaññako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3385\. Saddasaññakattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.
