# 10.9 Veyyāvaccakattheraapadāna

* Sudhāvagga

* Veyyāvaccakattheraapadāna

(51.)

1831\. _“Vipassissa bhagavato,_  
_mahāpūgagaṇo ahu;_  
_Veyyāvaccakaro āsiṁ,_  
_sabbakiccesu vāvaṭo._  

(52.)

1832\. _Deyyadhammo ca me natthi,_  
_sugatassa mahesino;_  
_Avandiṁ satthuno pāde,_  
_vippasannena cetasā._  

(53.)

1833\. _Ekanavutito kappe,_  
_veyyāvaccaṁ akāsahaṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_veyyāvaccassidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(54.)

1834\. _Ito ca aṭṭhame kappe,_  
_rājā āsiṁ sucintito;_  
_Sattaratanasampanno,_  
_cakkavattī mahabbalo._  

(55.)

1835\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

1836\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā veyyāvaccako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

1837\. Veyyāvaccakattherassāpadānaṁ navamaṁ.
