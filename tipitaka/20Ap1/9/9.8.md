# 9.8 Saddasaññakattheraapadāna

* Timiravagga

* Saddasaññakattheraapadāna

(43.)

1742\. _“Pabbate himavantamhi,_  
_vasāmi paṇṇasanthare;_  
_Phussassa dhammaṁ bhaṇato,_  
_sadde cittaṁ pasādayiṁ._  

(44.)

1743\. _Dvenavute ito kappe,_  
_yaṁ kammamakariṁ tadā;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_puññakammassidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(45.)

1744\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

1745\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā saddasaññako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

1746\. Saddasaññakattherassāpadānaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
