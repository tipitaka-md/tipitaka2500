# 39.10.7

* Avaṭaphalavagga

* Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

(84.)

4278\. _Rājāhaṁ patthivo āsiṁ,_  
_sattiyā purisaṁ haniṁ;_  
_Tena kammavipākena,_  
_niraye paccisaṁ bhusaṁ._  

(85.)

4279\. _Kammuno tassa sesena,_  
_idāni sakalaṁ mama;_  
_Pāde chaviṁ pakappesi,_  
_na hi kammaṁ vinassati._
