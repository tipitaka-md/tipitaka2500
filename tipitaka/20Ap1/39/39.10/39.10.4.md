# 39.10.4

* Avaṭaphalavagga

* Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

(78.)

4272\. _Vemātubhātaraṁ pubbe,_  
_dhanahetu haniṁ ahaṁ;_  
_Pakkhipiṁ giriduggasmiṁ,_  
_silāya ca apiṁsayiṁ._  

(79.)

4273\. _Tena kammavipākena,_  
_devadatto silaṁ khipi;_  
_Aṅguṭṭhaṁ piṁsayī pāde,_  
_mama pāsāṇasakkharā._
