# 39.10.8

* Avaṭaphalavagga

* Pubbakammapilotikabuddhaapadāna

(86.)

4280\. _Ahaṁ kevaṭṭagāmasmiṁ,_  
_ahuṁ kevaṭṭadārako;_  
_Macchake ghātite disvā,_  
_janayiṁ somanassakaṁ._  

(87.)

4281\. _Tena kammavipākena,_  
_sīsadukkhaṁ ahū mama;_  
_Sabbe sakkā ca haññiṁsu,_  
_yadā hani viṭaṭūbho._
