# 37.8 Ekacampakapupphiyattheraapadāna

* Mandāravapupphiyavagga

* Ekacampakapupphiyattheraapadāna

(27.)

4073\. _“Upasanto ca sambuddho,_  
_vasatī pabbatantare;_  
_Ekacampakamādāya,_  
_upagacchiṁ naruttamaṁ._  

(28.)

4074\. _Pasannacitto sumano,_  
_paccekamunimuttamaṁ;_  
_Ubhohatthehi paggayha,_  
_pūjayiṁ aparājitaṁ._  

(29.)

4075\. _Pañcasaṭṭhimhito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(30.)

4076\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

4077\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā ekacampakapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

4078\. Ekacampakapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
