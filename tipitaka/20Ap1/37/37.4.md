# 37.4 Kesarapupphiyattheraapadāna

* Mandāravapupphiyavagga

* Kesarapupphiyattheraapadāna

(12.)

4050\. _“Vijjādharo tadā āsiṁ,_  
_himavantamhi pabbate;_  
_Addasaṁ virajaṁ buddhaṁ,_  
_caṅkamantaṁ mahāyasaṁ._  

(13.)

4051\. _Tīṇi kesarapupphāni,_  
_sīse katvānahaṁ tadā;_  
_Upasaṅkamma sambuddhaṁ,_  
_vessabhuṁ abhipūjayiṁ._  

(14.)

4052\. _Ekattiṁse ito kappe,_  
_yaṁ kammamakariṁ tadā;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(15.)

4053\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

4054\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā kesarapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

4055\. Kesarapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.
