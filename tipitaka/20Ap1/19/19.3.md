# 19.3 Koṭumbariyattheraapadāna

* Kuṭajapupphiyavagga

* Koṭumbariyattheraapadāna

(12.)

2596\. _“Kaṇikāraṁva jotantaṁ,_  
_nisinnaṁ pabbatantare;_  
_Appameyyaṁva udadhiṁ,_  
_vitthataṁ dharaṇiṁ yathā._  

(13.)

2597\. _Pūjitaṁ devasaṅghena,_  
_nisabhājāniyaṁ yathā;_  
_Haṭṭho haṭṭhena cittena,_  
_upāgacchiṁ naruttamaṁ._  

(14.)

2598\. _Sattapupphāni paggayha,_  
_koṭumbarasamākulaṁ;_  
_Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,_  
_sikhino lokabandhuno._  

(15.)

2599\. _Ekattiṁse ito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(16.)

2600\. _Ito vīsatikappamhi,_  
_mahānelasanāmako;_  
_Eko āsi mahātejo,_  
_cakkavattī mahabbalo._  

(17.)

2601\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

2602\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā koṭumbariyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2603\. Koṭumbariyattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.
