# 18.3 Rattipupphiyattheraapadāna

* Kumudavagga

* Rattipupphiyattheraapadāna

(13.)

2526\. _“Migaluddo pure āsiṁ,_  
_araññe kānane ahaṁ;_  
_Vipassiṁ addasaṁ buddhaṁ,_  
_devadevaṁ narāsabhaṁ._  

(14.)

2527\. _Rattikaṁ pupphitaṁ disvā,_  
_kuṭajaṁ dharaṇīruhaṁ;_  
_Samūlaṁ paggahetvāna,_  
_upanesiṁ mahesino._  

(15.)

2528\. _Ekanavutito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_pupphadānassidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(16.)

2529\. _Ito ca aṭṭhame kappe,_  
_suppasannasanāmako;_  
_Sattaratanasampanno,_  
_rājāhosiṁ mahabbalo._  

(17.)

2530\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

2531\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā rattipupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2532\. Rattipupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.
