# 13.9 Gaṇṭhipupphiyattheraapadāna

* Sereyyavagga

* Gaṇṭhipupphiyattheraapadāna

(91.)

2160\. _“Suvaṇṇavaṇṇo sambuddho,_  
_vipassī dakkhiṇāraho;_  
_Purakkhato sāvakehi,_  
_ārāmā abhinikkhami._  

(92.)

2161\. _Disvānahaṁ buddhaseṭṭhaṁ,_  
_sabbaññuṁ tamanāsakaṁ;_  
_Pasannacitto sumano,_  
_gaṇṭhipupphaṁ apūjayiṁ._  

(93.)

2162\. _Tena cittappasādena,_  
_dvipadindassa tādino;_  
_Haṭṭho haṭṭhena cittena,_  
_puna vandiṁ tathāgataṁ._  

(94.)

2163\. _Ekanavutito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(95.)

2164\. _Ekatālīsito kappe,_  
_caraṇo nāma khattiyo;_  
_Sattaratanasampanno,_  
_cakkavattī mahabbalo._  

(96.)

2165\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

2166\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā gaṇṭhipupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2167\. Gaṇṭhipupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ navamaṁ.
