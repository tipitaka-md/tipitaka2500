# 23.7 Sovaṇṇavaṭaṃsakiyattheraapadāna

* Ālambaṇadāyakavagga

* Sovaṇṇavaṭaṃsakiyattheraapadāna

(34.)

2944\. _“Uyyānabhūmiṁ niyyanto,_  
_addasaṁ lokanāyakaṁ;_  
_Vaṭaṁsakaṁ gahetvāna,_  
_sovaṇṇaṁ sādhunimmitaṁ._  

(35.)

2945\. _Sīghaṁ tato samāruyha,_  
_hatthikkhandhagato ahaṁ;_  
_Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,_  
_sikhino lokabandhuno._  

(36.)

2946\. _Ekattiṁse ito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhiropayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_pupphapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(37.)

2947\. _Sattavīse ito kappe,_  
_eko āsiṁ janādhipo;_  
_Mahāpatāpanāmena,_  
_cakkavattī mahabbalo._  

(38.)

2948\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

2949\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā sovaṇṇavaṭaṁsakiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2950\. Sovaṇṇavaṭaṁsakiyattherassāpadānaṁ sattamaṁ.
