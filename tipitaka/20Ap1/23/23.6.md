# 23.6 Aṅkolapupphiyattheraapadāna

* Ālambaṇadāyakavagga

* Aṅkolapupphiyattheraapadāna

(29.)

2937\. _“Nārado iti me nāmaṁ,_  
_kassapo iti maṁ vidū;_  
_Addasaṁ samaṇānaggaṁ,_  
_vipassiṁ devasakkataṁ._  

(30.)

2938\. _Anubyañjanadharaṁ buddhaṁ,_  
_āhutīnaṁ paṭiggahaṁ;_  
_Aṅkolapupphaṁ paggayha,_  
_buddhassa abhiropayiṁ._  

(31.)

2939\. _Ekanavutito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(32.)

2940\. _Catusattatito kappe,_  
_romaso nāma khattiyo;_  
_Āmukkamālābharaṇo,_  
_sayoggabalavāhano._  

(33.)

2941\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

2942\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā aṅkolapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

2943\. Aṅkolapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
