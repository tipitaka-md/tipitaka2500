# 38.6 Gandhamuṭṭhiyattheraapadāna

* Bodhivandanavagga

* Gandhamuṭṭhiyattheraapadāna

(33.)

4138\. _“Citake karīyamāne,_  
_nānāgandhe samāhate;_  
_Pasannacitto sumano,_  
_gandhamuṭṭhimapūjayiṁ._  

(34.)

4139\. _Satasahassito kappe,_  
_citakaṁ yaṁ apūjayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_citapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(35.)

4140\. _Kilesā jhāpitā mayhaṁ,_  
_…pe…_  
_viharāmi anāsavo._  

(36.)

4141\. _Svāgataṁ vata me āsi,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ._  

(37.)

4142\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

4143\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā gandhamuṭṭhiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

4144\. Gandhamuṭṭhiyattherassāpadānaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
