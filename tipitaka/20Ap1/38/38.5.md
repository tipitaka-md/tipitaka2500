# 38.5 Sattapaṇṇiyattheraapadāna

* Bodhivandanavagga

* Sattapaṇṇiyattheraapadāna

(28.)

4131\. _“Sumano nāma sambuddho,_  
_uppajji lokanāyako;_  
_Pasannacitto sumano,_  
_sattapaṇṇimapūjayiṁ._  

(29.)

4132\. _Satasahassito kappe,_  
_Sattapaṇṇimapūjayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_Sattapaṇṇipūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(30.)

4133\. _Kilesā jhāpitā mayhaṁ,_  
_…pe…_  
_viharāmi anāsavo._  

(31.)

4134\. _Svāgataṁ vata me āsi,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ._  

(32.)

4135\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

4136\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā sattapaṇṇiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

4137\. Sattapaṇṇiyattherassāpadānaṁ pañcamaṁ.
