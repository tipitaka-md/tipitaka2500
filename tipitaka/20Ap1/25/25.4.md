# 25.4 Viravapupphiyattheraapadāna

* Tuvaradāyakavagga

* Viravapupphiyattheraapadāna

(14.)

3053\. _“Khīṇāsavasahassehi,_  
_niyyāti lokanāyako;_  
_Viravapupphamādāya,_  
_buddhassa abhiropayiṁ._  

(15.)

3054\. _Ekanavutito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(16.)

3055\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

3056\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā viravapupphiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3057\. Viravapupphiyattherassāpadānaṁ catutthaṁ.
