# 25.8 Sattalipupphapūjakattheraapadāna

* Tuvaradāyakavagga

* Sattalipupphapūjakattheraapadāna

(27.)

3074\. _“Satta sattalipupphāni,_  
_sīse katvānahaṁ tadā;_  
_Buddhassa abhiropesiṁ,_  
_vessabhumhi naruttame._  

(28.)

3075\. _Ekattiṁse ito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(29.)

3076\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

3077\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā sattalipupphapūjako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3078\. Sattalipupphapūjakattherassāpadānaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
