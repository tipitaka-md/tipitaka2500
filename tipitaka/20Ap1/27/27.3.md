# 27.3 Aḍḍhacandiyattheraapadāna

* Padumukkhipavagga

* Aḍḍhacandiyattheraapadāna

(10.)

3171\. _“Tissassa kho bhagavato,_  
_bodhiyā pādaputtame;_  
_Aḍḍhacandaṁ mayā dinnaṁ,_  
_dharaṇīruhapādape._  

(11.)

3172\. _Dvenavute ito kappe,_  
_yaṁ candamabhiropayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_bodhipūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(12.)

3173\. _Pañcavīse ito kappe,_  
_devalo nāma khattiyo;_  
_Sattaratanasampanno,_  
_cakkavattī mahabbalo._  

(13.)

3174\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

3175\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā aḍḍhacandiyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

3176\. Aḍḍhacandiyattherassāpadānaṁ tatiyaṁ.
