# 53.7 Girinelapūjakattheraapadāna

* Tiṇadāyakavagga

* Girinelapūjakattheraapadāna

(42.)

6851\. _“Migaluddo pure āsiṁ,_  
_vipine vicaraṁ ahaṁ;_  
_Addasaṁ virajaṁ buddhaṁ,_  
_sabbadhammāna pāraguṁ._  

(43.)

6852\. _Tasmiṁ mahākāruṇike,_  
_sabbasattahite rate;_  
_Pasannacitto sumano,_  
_nelapupphamapūjayiṁ._  

(44.)

6853\. _Ekatiṁse ito kappe,_  
_yaṁ pupphamabhipūjayiṁ;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_buddhapūjāyidaṁ phalaṁ._  

(45.)

6854\. _Kilesā jhāpitā mayhaṁ,_  
_…pe…_  
_viharāmi anāsavo._  

(46.)

6855\. _Svāgataṁ vata me āsi,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ._  

(47.)

6856\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

6857\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā girinelapūjako thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

6858\. Girinelapūjakattherassāpadānaṁ sattamaṁ.
