# 8.9 Ṭhitañjaliyattheraapadāna

* Nāgasamālavagga

* Ṭhitañjaliyattheraapadāna

(42.)

1646\. _“Migaluddo pure āsiṁ,_  
_Araññe kānane ahaṁ;_  
_Tattha addasaṁ sambuddhaṁ,_  
_Bāttiṁsavaralakkhaṇaṁ._  

(43.)

1647\. _Tatthāhaṁ añjaliṁ katvā,_  
_pakkāmiṁ pācināmukho;_  
_Avidūre nisinnassa,_  
_niyake paṇṇasanthare._  

(44.)

1648\. _Tato me asanīpāto,_  
_matthake nipatī tadā;_  
_Sohaṁ maraṇakālamhi,_  
_akāsiṁ punarañjaliṁ._  

(45.)

1649\. _Dvenavute ito kappe,_  
_añjaliṁ akariṁ tadā;_  
_Duggatiṁ nābhijānāmi,_  
_añjalissa idaṁ phalaṁ._  

(46.)

1650\. _Catupaṇṇāsakappamhi,_  
_migaketusanāmako;_  
_Sattaratanasampanno,_  
_cakkavattī mahabbalo._  

(47.)

1651\. _Paṭisambhidā catasso,_  
_…pe…_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ”._  

1652\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā ṭhitañjaliyo thero imā gāthāyo abhāsitthāti.

1653\. Ṭhitañjaliyattherassāpadānaṁ navamaṁ.
