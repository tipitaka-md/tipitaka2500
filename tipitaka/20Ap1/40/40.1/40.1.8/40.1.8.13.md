# 40.1.8.13 Aṃsabaddhānisaṃsa

* Pilindavacchavagga

* Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

* Dānānisaṃsakathā

* Aṃsabaddhānisaṃsa

(125.)

4422\. _Aṁsabaddhe jine datvā,_  
_saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;_  
_Tīṇānisaṁse anubhomi,_  
_kammānucchavike mama._  

(126.)

4423\. _Saddhamme gādhaṁ vindāmi,_  
_Sarāmi dutiyaṁ bhavaṁ;_  
_Sabbattha succhavī homi,_  
_Tassa nissandato ahaṁ._
