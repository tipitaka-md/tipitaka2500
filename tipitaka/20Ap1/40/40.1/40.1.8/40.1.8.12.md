# 40.1.8.12 Sūcigharānisaṃsa

* Pilindavacchavagga

* Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

* Dānānisaṃsakathā

* Sūcigharānisaṃsa

(123.)

4420\. _Sūcighare sugate datvā,_  
_saṁghe gaṇavaruttame;_  
_Tīṇānisaṁse anubhomi,_  
_kammānucchavike mama._  

(124.)

4421\. _Cetosukhaṁ kāyasukhaṁ,_  
_iriyāpathajaṁ sukhaṁ;_  
_Ime guṇe paṭilabhe,_  
_tassa nissandato ahaṁ._
