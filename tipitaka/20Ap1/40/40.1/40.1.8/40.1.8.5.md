# 40.1.8.5 Satthakānisaṃsa

* Pilindavacchavagga

* Pilindavacchattheraapadāna

* Dānānisaṃsakathā

* Satthakānisaṃsa

(106.)

4403\. _Akakkase apharuse,_  
_sudhote satthake bahū;_  
_Pasannacitto datvāna,_  
_buddhe saṁghe tatheva ca._  

(107.)

4404\. _Pañcānisaṁse anubhomi,_  
_kammānucchavike mama;_  
_Kalyāṇamittaṁ vīriyaṁ,_  
_khantiñca mettasatthakaṁ._  

(108.)

4405\. _Taṇhāsallassa chinnattā,_  
_paññāsatthaṁ anuttaraṁ;_  
_Vajirena samaṁ ñāṇaṁ,_  
_tesaṁ nissandato labhe._
