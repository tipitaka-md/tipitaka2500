# 5.1.7 Yāgusutta

* Pañcamapaṇṇāsaka

* Kimilavagga

* Yāgusutta

(207.)

1208\. “Pañcime, bhikkhave, ānisaṁsā yāguyā. Katame pañca? Khuddaṁ paṭihanati, pipāsaṁ paṭivineti, vātaṁ anulometi, vatthiṁ sodheti, āmāvasesaṁ pāceti. Ime kho, bhikkhave, pañca ānisaṁsā yāguyā”ti.

1209\. Sattamaṁ.
