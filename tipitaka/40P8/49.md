# 49 Kilesaduka

* Kilesaduka

(68.)

210\. Nokilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nokileso dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Nokilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nanokileso dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Nokilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nokileso ca nanokileso ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (3)

211\. Nanokilesaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nanokileso dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

212\. Nokilesañca nanokilesañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nokileso dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

213\. Hetuyā nava…pe… vipāke ekaṁ…pe… avigate nava. (Sabbattha nava.)
