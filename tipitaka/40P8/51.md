# 51 Saṃkiliṭṭhaduka

* Saṃkiliṭṭhaduka

(70.)

218\. Nasaṁkiliṭṭhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasaṁkiliṭṭho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (1)

219\. Naasaṁkiliṭṭhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca naasaṁkiliṭṭho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Naasaṁkiliṭṭhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasaṁkiliṭṭho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Naasaṁkiliṭṭhaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nasaṁkiliṭṭho ca naasaṁkiliṭṭho ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (3)

220\. Nasaṁkiliṭṭhañca naasaṁkiliṭṭhañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nasaṁkiliṭṭho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (1) (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

221\. Hetuyā pañca, ārammaṇe dve…pe… vipāke ekaṁ…pe… avigate pañca. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)
