# 12.1--7 Paṭiccādivāra

* Lokiyaduka

* Paṭiccādivāra

(26.)

66\. Nalokiyaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nalokiyo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Nalokiyaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nalokuttaro dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. Nalokiyaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nalokiyo ca nalokuttaro ca dhammā uppajjanti hetupaccayā. (3)

67\. Nalokuttaraṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nalokuttaro dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā.

68\. Nalokiyañca nalokuttarañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nalokuttaro dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (2) (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

69\. Hetuyā pañca, ārammaṇe dve…pe… avigate pañca. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)
