# 1.7.6 Paṭippassambhetabbatecattālīsaka

* Kammakkhandhaka

* Pāpikāyadiṭṭhiyāappaṭinissagge ukkhepanīyakamma

* Paṭippassambhetabbatecattālīsaka

(73.)

329\. Pañcahi, bhikkhave, aṅgehi samannāgatassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambhetabbaṁ. Na upasampādeti, na nissayaṁ deti, na sāmaṇeraṁ upaṭṭhāpeti, na bhikkhunovādakasammutiṁ sādiyati, sammatopi bhikkhuniyo na ovadati— imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahaṅgehi samannāgatassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambhetabbaṁ.

330\. Aparehipi, bhikkhave, pañcahaṅgehi samannāgatassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambhetabbaṁ. Yāya āpattiyā saṁghena, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ kataṁ hoti taṁ āpattiṁ na āpajjati, aññaṁ vā tādisikaṁ, tato vā pāpiṭṭhataraṁ; kammaṁ na garahati, kammike na garahati— imehi kho, bhikkhave, pañcahaṅgehi samannāgatassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambhetabbaṁ…pe… .

331\. Aṭṭhahi, bhikkhave, aṅgehi samannāgatassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambhetabbaṁ. Na pakatattassa bhikkhuno uposathaṁ ṭhapeti, na pavāraṇaṁ ṭhapeti, na savacanīyaṁ karoti, na anuvādaṁ paṭṭhapeti, na okāsaṁ kāreti, na codeti, na sāreti, na bhikkhūhi sampayojeti— imehi kho, bhikkhave, aṭṭhahaṅgehi samannāgatassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambhetabbaṁ.

332\. Pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge ukkhepanīyakamme paṭippassambhetabbatecattālīsakaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ.

(74.)

333\. Evañca pana, bhikkhave, paṭippassambhetabbaṁ. Tena, bhikkhave, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammakatena bhikkhunā saṁghaṁ upasaṅkamitvā ekaṁsaṁ uttarāsaṅgaṁ karitvā vuḍḍhānaṁ bhikkhūnaṁ pāde vanditvā ukkuṭikaṁ nisīditvā añjaliṁ paggahetvā evamassa vacanīyo— ‘ahaṁ, bhante, saṁghena, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammakato sammā vattāmi, lomaṁ pātemi, netthāraṁ vattāmi, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyassa kammassa paṭippassaddhiṁ yācāmī’ti. Dutiyampi yācitabbā. Tatiyampi yācitabbā. Byattena bhikkhunā paṭibalena saṁgho ñāpetabbo—

334\. ‘Suṇātu me, bhante, saṁgho. Ayaṁ itthannāmo bhikkhu saṁghena, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammakato sammā vattati, lomaṁ pāteti, netthāraṁ vattati, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyassa kammassa paṭippassaddhiṁ yācati. Yadi saṁghassa pattakallaṁ, saṁgho itthannāmassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambheyya. Esā ñatti.

335\. Suṇātu me, bhante, saṁgho. Ayaṁ itthannāmo bhikkhu saṁghena, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammakato sammā vattati, lomaṁ pāteti, netthāraṁ vattati, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyassa kammassa paṭippassaddhiṁ yācati. Saṁgho itthannāmassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambheti. Yassāyasmato khamati itthannāmassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyassa kammassa paṭippassaddhi, so tuṇhassa; yassa nakkhamati, so bhāseyya.

336\. Dutiyampi etamatthaṁ vadāmi…pe… tatiyampi etamatthaṁ vadāmi— suṇātu me, bhante, saṁgho. Ayaṁ itthannāmo bhikkhu saṁghena, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammakato sammā vattati, lomaṁ pāteti, netthāraṁ vattati, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge ukkhepanīyassa kammassa paṭippassaddhiṁ yācati. Saṁgho itthannāmassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ paṭippassambheti. Yassāyasmato khamati itthannāmassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyassa kammassa paṭippassaddhi, so tuṇhassa; yassa nakkhamati, so bhāseyya.

337\. Paṭippassaddhaṁ saṁghena itthannāmassa bhikkhuno, pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge, ukkhepanīyakammaṁ. Khamati saṁghassa, tasmā tuṇhī, evametaṁ dhārayāmī’”ti.

338\. Pāpikāya diṭṭhiyā appaṭinissagge ukkhepanīyakammaṁ niṭṭhitaṁ sattamaṁ. Kammakkhandhako paṭhamo. Imamhi khandhake vatthū satta.

339\. 

340\. _Paṇḍulohitakā bhikkhū,_  
_sayaṁ bhaṇḍanakārakā;_  
_Tādise upasaṅkamma,_  
_ussahiṁsu ca bhaṇḍane._  

341\. _Anuppannāpi jāyanti,_  
_uppannānipi vaḍḍhare;_  
_Appicchā pesalā bhikkhū,_  
_ujjhāyanti padassato._  

342\. _Saddhammaṭṭhitiko buddho,_  
_sayambhū aggapuggalo;_  
_Āṇāpesi tajjanīya,_  
_kammaṁ sāvatthiyaṁ jino._  

343\. _Asammukhāppaṭipucchāp-_  
_paṭiññāya katañca yaṁ;_  
_Anāpatti adesane,_  
_desitāya katañca yaṁ._  

344\. _Acodetvā asāretvā,_  
_anāropetvā ca yaṁ kataṁ;_  
_Asammukhā adhammena,_  
_vaggena cāpi yaṁ kataṁ._  

345\. _Appaṭipucchā adhammena,_  
_puna vaggena yaṁ kataṁ;_  
_Appaṭiññāya adhammena,_  
_vaggena cāpi yaṁ kataṁ._  

346\. _Anāpatti adhammena,_  
_vaggena cāpi yaṁ kataṁ;_  
_Adesanāgāminiyā,_  
_adhammavaggameva ca._  

347\. _Desitāya adhammena,_  
_vaggenāpi tatheva ca;_  
_Acodetvā adhammena,_  
_vaggenāpi tatheva ca._  

348\. _Asāretvā adhammena,_  
_vaggenāpi tatheva ca;_  
_Anāropetvā adhammena,_  
_vaggenāpi tatheva ca._  

349\. _Kaṇhavāranayeneva,_  
_sukkavāraṁ vijāniyā;_  
_Saṁgho ākaṅkhamāno ca,_  
_yassa tajjanīyaṁ kare._  

350\. _Bhaṇḍanaṁ bālo saṁsaṭṭho,_  
_adhisīle ajjhācāre;_  
_Atidiṭṭhivipannassa,_  
_saṁgho tajjanīyaṁ kare._  

351\. _Buddhadhammassa saṁghassa,_  
_avaṇṇaṁ yo ca bhāsati;_  
_Tiṇṇannampi ca bhikkhūnaṁ,_  
_saṁgho tajjaniyaṁ kare._  

352\. _Bhaṇḍanaṁ kārako eko,_  
_bālo saṁsagganissito;_  
_Adhisīle ajjhācāre,_  
_tatheva atidiṭṭhiyā._  

353\. _Buddhadhammassa saṁghassa,_  
_avaṇṇaṁ yo ca bhāsati;_  
_Tajjanīyakammakato,_  
_evaṁ sammānuvattanā._  

354\. _Upasampadanissayā,_  
_sāmaṇeraṁ upaṭṭhanā;_  
_Ovādasammatenāpi,_  
_na kare tajjanīkato._  

355\. _Nāpajje tañca āpattiṁ,_  
_tādisañca tato paraṁ;_  
_Kammañca kammike cāpi,_  
_na garahe tathāvidho._  

356\. _Uposathaṁ pavāraṇaṁ,_  
_pakatattassa naṭṭhape;_  
_Savacaniṁ anuvādo,_  
_okāso codanena ca._  

357\. _Sāraṇaṁ sampayogañca,_  
_na kareyya tathāvidho;_  
_Upasampadanissayā,_  
_sāmaṇeraṁ upaṭṭhanā._  

358\. _Ovādasammatenāpi,_  
_pañcahaṅgehi na sammati;_  
_Tañcāpajjati āpattiṁ,_  
_tādisañca tato paraṁ._  

359\. _Kammañca kammike cāpi,_  
_garahanto na sammati;_  
_Uposathaṁ pavāraṇaṁ,_  
_savacanīyā ca novādo._  

360\. _Okāso codanañceva,_  
_sāraṇā sampayojanā;_  
_Imehaṭṭhaṅgehi yo yutto,_  
_tajjanānupasammati._  

361\. _Kaṇhavāranayeneva,_  
_sukkavāraṁ vijāniyā;_  
_Bālo āpattibahulo,_  
_saṁsaṭṭhopi ca seyyaso._  

362\. _Niyassakammaṁ sambuddho,_  
_āṇāpesi mahāmuni;_  
_Kīṭāgirismiṁ dve bhikkhū,_  
_assajipunabbasukā._  

363\. _Anācārañca vividhaṁ,_  
_ācariṁsu asaññatā;_  
_Pabbājanīyaṁ sambuddho,_  
_kammaṁ sāvatthiyaṁ jino;_  
_Macchikāsaṇḍe sudhammo,_  
_cittassāvāsiko ahu._  

364\. _Jātivādena khuṁseti,_  
_sudhammo cittupāsakaṁ;_  
_Paṭisāraṇīyakammaṁ,_  
_āṇāpesi tathāgato._  

365\. _Kosambiyaṁ channaṁ bhikkhuṁ,_  
_Nicchantāpattiṁ passituṁ;_  
_Adassane ukkhipituṁ,_  
_Āṇāpesi jinuttamo._  

366\. _Channo taṁyeva āpattiṁ,_  
_paṭikātuṁ na icchati;_  
_Ukkhepanāppaṭikamme,_  
_āṇāpesi vināyako._  

367\. _Pāpadiṭṭhi ariṭṭhassa,_  
_āsi aññāṇanissitā;_  
_Diṭṭhiyāppaṭinissagge,_  
_ukkhepaṁ jinabhāsitaṁ._  

368\. _Niyassakammaṁ pabbajjaṁ,_  
_tatheva paṭisāraṇī;_  
_Adassanāppaṭikamme,_  
_anissagge ca diṭṭhiyā._  

369\. _Davānācārūpaghāti,_  
_micchāājīvameva ca;_  
_Pabbājanīyakammamhi,_  
_atirekapadā ime._  

370\. _Alābhāvaṇṇā dve pañca,_  
_dve pañcakāti nāmakā;_  
_Paṭisāraṇīyakammamhi,_  
_atirekapadā ime._  

371\. _Tajjanīyaṁ niyassañca,_  
_duve kammāpi sādisā;_  
_Pabbajjā paṭisārī ca,_  
_atthi padātirittatā._  

372\. _Tayo ukkhepanā kammā,_  
_sadisā te vibhattito;_  
_Tajjanīyanayenāpi,_  
_sesakammaṁ vijāniyāti._  

373\. Kammakkhandhako niṭṭhito.
