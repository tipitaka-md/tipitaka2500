# 4.6.2 Dhammakammadvādasaka

* Samathakkhandhaka

* Tassapāpiyasikā

* Dhammakammadvādasaka

(209.)

862\. Tīhi, bhikkhave, aṅgehi samannāgataṁ tassapāpiyasikākammaṁ dhammakammañca hoti, vinayakammañca, suvūpasantañca. Sammukhā kataṁ hoti, paṭipucchākataṁ hoti, paṭiññāya kataṁ hoti…pe… dhammena kataṁ hoti, samaggena kataṁ hoti— imehi kho, bhikkhave, tīhaṅgehi samannāgataṁ tassapāpiyasikākammaṁ dhammakammañca hoti, vinayakammañca, suvūpasantañca.
