# Navippayutta

* Kusalattika

* Saṃsaṭṭhavāra

* Paccayapaccanīya

* Vibhaṅgavāra

* Navippayutta

(222.)

725\. Kusalaṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho kusalo dhammo uppajjati navippayuttapaccayā— arūpe kusalaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ saṁsaṭṭhā tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhe saṁsaṭṭhā dve khandhā. (1)

726\. Akusalaṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho akusalo dhammo uppajjati navippayuttapaccayā— arūpe akusalaṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ saṁsaṭṭhā tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhe saṁsaṭṭhā dve khandhā. (1)

727\. Abyākataṁ dhammaṁ saṁsaṭṭho abyākato dhammo uppajjati navippayuttapaccayā— arūpe vipākābyākataṁ kiriyābyākataṁ ekaṁ khandhaṁ saṁsaṭṭhā tayo khandhā…pe… dve khandhe saṁsaṭṭhā dve khandhā. (Navippayutte paṭisandhi natthi.) (1)
