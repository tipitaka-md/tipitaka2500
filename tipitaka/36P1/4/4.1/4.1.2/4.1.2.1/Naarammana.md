# Naārammaṇa

* Vipākattika

* Paṭiccavāra

* Paccayapaccanīya

* Vibhaṅgavāra

* Naārammaṇa

(27.)

2100\. Vipākaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā— vipāke khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ; paṭisandhikkhaṇe vipāke khandhe paṭicca kaṭattārūpaṁ, khandhe paṭicca vatthu. (1)

(28.)

2101\. Vipākadhammadhammaṁ paṭicca nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā— vipākadhammadhamme khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (1)

(29.)

2102\. Nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammaṁ paṭicca nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā— nevavipākanavipākadhammadhamme khandhe paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ, ekaṁ mahābhūtaṁ paṭicca tayo mahābhūtā…pe… mahābhūte paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ kaṭattārūpaṁ upādārūpaṁ; bāhiraṁ… āhārasamuṭṭhānaṁ… utusamuṭṭhānaṁ… asaññasattānaṁ ekaṁ mahābhūtaṁ paṭicca tayo mahābhūtā… mahābhūte paṭicca kaṭattārūpaṁ upādārūpaṁ. (1)

(30.)

2103\. Vipākañca nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā— vipāke khandhe ca mahābhūte ca paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ; paṭisandhikkhaṇe vipāke khandhe ca mahābhūte ca paṭicca kaṭattārūpaṁ. (1)

(31.)

2104\. Vipākadhammadhammañca nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammañca dhammaṁ paṭicca nevavipākanavipākadhammadhammo uppajjati naārammaṇapaccayā— vipākadhammadhamme khandhe ca mahābhūte ca paṭicca cittasamuṭṭhānaṁ rūpaṁ. (1)
