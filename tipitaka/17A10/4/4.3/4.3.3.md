# 4.3.3 Kusalasutta

* Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

* Sādhuvagga

* Kusalasutta

(180.)

1327\. “Kusalañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi akusalañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamañca, bhikkhave, akusalaṁ? Pāṇātipāto…pe… micchādiṭṭhi— idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, akusalaṁ.

1328\. Katamañca, bhikkhave, kusalaṁ? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī…pe… sammādiṭṭhi— idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, kusalan”ti.

1329\. Tatiyaṁ.
