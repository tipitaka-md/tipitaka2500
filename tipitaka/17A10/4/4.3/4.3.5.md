# 4.3.5 Dhammasutta

* Catutthapaṇṇāsaka

* Sādhuvagga

* Dhammasutta

(182.)

1333\. “Dhammañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi adhammañca. Taṁ suṇātha…pe… katamo ca, bhikkhave, adhammo? Pāṇātipāto…pe… micchādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, adhammo.

1334\. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, dhammo? Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī…pe… sammādiṭṭhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, dhammo”ti.

1335\. Pañcamaṁ.
