# 2.5.10 Abhabbasutta

* Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

* Upālivagga

* Abhabbasutta

(100.)

918\. “Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhamme appahāya abhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. Katame dasa? Rāgaṁ, dosaṁ, mohaṁ, kodhaṁ, upanāhaṁ, makkhaṁ, paḷāsaṁ, issaṁ, macchariyaṁ, mānaṁ— ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhamme appahāya abhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ.

919\. Dasayime, bhikkhave, dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātuṁ. Katame dasa? Rāgaṁ, dosaṁ, mohaṁ, kodhaṁ, upanāhaṁ, makkhaṁ, paḷāsaṁ, issaṁ, macchariyaṁ, mānaṁ— ime kho, bhikkhave, dasa dhamme pahāya bhabbo arahattaṁ sacchikātun”ti.

920\. Dasamaṁ.

921\. Upālivaggo pañcamo.

922\. 

923\. _Kāmabhogī bhayaṁ diṭṭhi,_  
_vajjiyamāhituttiyā;_  
_Kokanudo āhuneyyo,_  
_thero upāli abhabboti._  

924\. Dutiyo paṇṇāsako samatto.
