# 2.2.10 Dutiyatajjhānasutta

* Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

* Devatāvagga

* Dutiyatajjhānasutta

(74.)

647\. “Cha, bhikkhave, dhamme appahāya abhabbo paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharituṁ. Katame cha? Kāmavitakkaṁ, byāpādavitakkaṁ, vihiṁsāvitakkaṁ, kāmasaññaṁ, byāpādasaññaṁ, vihiṁsāsaññaṁ— ime kho, bhikkhave, cha dhamme appahāya abhabbo paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharituṁ.

648\. Cha, bhikkhave, dhamme pahāya bhabbo paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharituṁ. Katame cha? Kāmavitakkaṁ, byāpādavitakkaṁ, vihiṁsāvitakkaṁ, kāmasaññaṁ, byāpādasaññaṁ, vihiṁsāsaññaṁ— ime kho, bhikkhave, cha dhamme pahāya bhabbo paṭhamaṁ jhānaṁ upasampajja viharitun”ti.

649\. Dasamaṁ.

650\. Devatāvaggo sattamo.

651\. 

652\. _Anāgāmi arahaṁ mittā,_  
_saṅgaṇikārāmadevatā;_  
_Samādhisakkhibhabbaṁ balaṁ,_  
_tajjhānā apare duveti._
