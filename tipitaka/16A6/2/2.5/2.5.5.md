# 2.5.5 Anattasutta

* Dutiyapaṇṇāsaka

* Ānisaṃsavagga

* Anattasutta

(100.)

723\. “So vata, bhikkhave, bhikkhu kañci dhammaṁ attato samanupassanto…pe… sabbadhamme anattato samanupassanto…pe… ṭhānametaṁ vijjati”.

724\. Pañcamaṁ.
