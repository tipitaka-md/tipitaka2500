# 1 Saraṇattaya

* Saraṇattaya

(1.)

2\. _Buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi,_  
_Dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi,_  
_Saṁghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi._  

3\. _Dutiyampi buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi,_  
_Dutiyampi dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi,_  
_Dutiyampi saṁghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi._  

4\. _Tatiyampi buddhaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi,_  
_Tatiyampi dhammaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi,_  
_Tatiyampi saṁghaṁ saraṇaṁ gacchāmi._  

5\. Saraṇattayaṁ.
