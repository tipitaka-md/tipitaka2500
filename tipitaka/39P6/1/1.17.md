# 1.17 Hetuduka, āsavaāsavasampayuttaduka

* Hetuduka, Duka

* Hetuduka, āsavaāsavasampayuttaduka

(40.)

115\. Hetuṁ āsavañceva āsavasampayuttañca dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu āsavo ceva āsavasampayutto ca dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

116\. Hetuyā pañca…pe… avigate pañca. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)

(41.)

117\. Hetuṁ āsavasampayuttañceva no ca āsavaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu āsavasampayutto ceva no ca āsavo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā.

118\. Hetuyā pañca…pe… avigate pañca. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)
