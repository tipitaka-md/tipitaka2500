# 1.31 Hetuduka, Ajjhattikaduka

* Hetuduka, Duka

* Hetuduka, Ajjhattikaduka

(55.)

156\. Nahetuṁ ajjhattikaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu ajjhattiko dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Sabbattha ekaṁ. Sabbattha vitthāro.)

157\. Hetuṁ bāhiraṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu bāhiro dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Sabbattha nava. Sabbattha vitthāro.)
