# 1.20 Hetuduka, Sārammaṇaduka

* Hetuduka, Duka

* Hetuduka, Sārammaṇaduka

(44.)

127\. Hetuṁ sārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu sārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

128\. Nahetuṁ sārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu sārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi.

129\. Hetuṁ sārammaṇañca nahetuṁ sārammaṇañca dhammaṁ paṭicca hetu sārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… tīṇi. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

130\. Hetuyā nava…pe… avigate nava. (Sabbattha vitthāro.)

131\. Nahetuṁ anārammaṇaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca nahetu anārammaṇo dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Sabbattha ekaṁ.)
