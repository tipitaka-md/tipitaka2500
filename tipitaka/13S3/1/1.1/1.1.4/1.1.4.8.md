# 1.1.4.8 Dutiyaanudhammasutta

* Khandhasaṃyutta

* Mūlapaṇṇāsaka

* Natumhākavagga

* Dutiyaanudhammasutta

(40.)

183\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Dhammānudhammappaṭipannassa, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno ayamanudhammo hoti yaṁ rūpe aniccānupassī vihareyya…pe… parimuccati dukkhasmāti vadāmī”ti.

184\. Aṭṭhamaṁ.
