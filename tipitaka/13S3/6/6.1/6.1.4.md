# 6.1.4 Samphassasutta

* Kilesasaṃyutta

* Kilesavagga

* Samphassasutta

(325.)

1099\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Yo, bhikkhave, cakkhusamphassasmiṁ chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. Yo sotasamphassasmiṁ… yo ghānasamphassasmiṁ… yo jivhāsamphassasmiṁ… yo kāyasamphassasmiṁ… yo manosamphassasmiṁ chandarāgo, cittasseso upakkileso. Yato kho, bhikkhave, bhikkhuno…pe… abhiññā sacchikaraṇīyesu dhammesū”ti.

1100\. Catutthaṁ.
