# 7.1.6 Viññāṇañcāyatanasutta

* Sāriputtasaṃyutta

* Sāriputtavagga

* Viññāṇañcāyatanasutta

(337.)

1132\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. Addasā kho āyasmā ānando…pe… “idhāhaṁ, āvuso, sabbaso ākāsānañcāyatanaṁ samatikkamma anantaṁ viññāṇanti viññāṇañcāyatanaṁ upasampajja viharāmi…pe… vuṭṭhitoti vā”ti.

1133\. Chaṭṭhaṁ.
