# 4.1.8 Rūpataṇhāsutta

* Okkantasaṃyutta

* Cakkhuvagga

* Rūpataṇhāsutta

(309.)

1057\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Rūpataṇhā, bhikkhave, aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī; saddataṇhā… gandhataṇhā… rasataṇhā… phoṭṭhabbataṇhā… dhammataṇhā aniccā vipariṇāmī aññathābhāvī. Yo, bhikkhave, ime dhamme evaṁ saddahati adhimuccati, ayaṁ vuccati ‘saddhānusārī…pe… sambodhiparāyano’”ti.

1058\. Aṭṭhamaṁ.
