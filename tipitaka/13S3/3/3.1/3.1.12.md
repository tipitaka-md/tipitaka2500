# 3.1.12 Anantavāsutta

* Diṭṭhisaṃyutta

* Sotāpattivagga

* Anantavāsutta

(217.)

959\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Kismiṁ nu kho, bhikkhave, sati, kiṁ upādāya, kiṁ abhinivissa evaṁ diṭṭhi uppajjati— ‘anantavā loko’”ti? “Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā…pe… niyato sambodhiparāyano”ti.

960\. Dvādasamaṁ.
