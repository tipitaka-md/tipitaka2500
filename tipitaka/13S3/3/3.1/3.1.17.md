# 3.1.17 Hoticanacahotitathāgatosutta

* Diṭṭhisaṃyutta

* Sotāpattivagga

* Hoticanacahotitathāgatosutta

(222.)

969\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Kismiṁ nu kho, bhikkhave, sati, kiṁ upādāya, kiṁ abhinivissa evaṁ diṭṭhi uppajjati— ‘hoti ca na ca hoti tathāgato paraṁ maraṇā’”ti? “Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā…pe… niyato sambodhiparāyano”ti.

970\. Sattarasamaṁ.
