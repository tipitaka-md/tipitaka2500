# 3.1.15 Hotitathāgatosutta

* Diṭṭhisaṃyutta

* Sotāpattivagga

* Hotitathāgatosutta

(220.)

965\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Kismiṁ nu kho, bhikkhave, sati, kiṁ upādāya, kiṁ abhinivissa evaṁ diṭṭhi uppajjati— ‘hoti tathāgato paraṁ maraṇā’”ti? “Bhagavaṁmūlakā no, bhante, dhammā…pe… niyato sambodhiparāyano”ti.

966\. Pannarasamaṁ.
