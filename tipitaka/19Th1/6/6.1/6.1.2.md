# 6.1.2 Tekicchakārittheragāthā

* Chakkanipāta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Tekicchakārittheragāthā

(381.)

650\. _“Atihitā vīhi,_  
_khalagatā sālī;_  
_Na ca labhe piṇḍaṁ,_  
_kathamahaṁ kassaṁ”._  

(382.)

651\. _“Buddhamappameyyaṁ anussara pasanno,_  
_Pītiyā phuṭasarīro hohisi satatamudaggo._  

(383.)

652\. _Dhammamappameyyaṁ anussara pasanno,_  
_Pītiyā phuṭasarīro hohisi satatamudaggo._  

(384.)

653\. _Saṁghamappameyyaṁ anussara pasanno,_  
_Pītiyā phuṭasarīro hohisi satatamudaggo”._  

(385.)

654\. _“Abbhokāse viharasi,_  
_Sītā hemantikā imā ratyo;_  
_Mā sītena pareto vihaññittho,_  
_Pavisa tvaṁ vihāraṁ phusitaggaḷaṁ”._  

(386.)

655\. _“Phusissaṁ catasso appamaññāyo,_  
_Tāhi ca sukhito viharissaṁ;_  
_Nāhaṁ sītena vihaññissaṁ,_  
_Aniñjito viharanto”ti._  

656\. … Tekicchakārī thero… .
