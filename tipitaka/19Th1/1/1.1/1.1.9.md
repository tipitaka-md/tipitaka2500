# 1.1.9 Pilindavacchattheragāthā

* Ekakanipāta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Pilindavacchattheragāthā

(9.)

21\. _“Svāgataṁ na durāgataṁ,_  
_Nayidaṁ dumantitaṁ mama;_  
_Saṁvibhattesu dhammesu,_  
_Yaṁ seṭṭhaṁ tadupāgamin”ti._  

22\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā pilindavaccho thero gāthaṁ abhāsitthāti.
