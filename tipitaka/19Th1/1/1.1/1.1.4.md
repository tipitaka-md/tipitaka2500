# 1.1.4 Puṇṇattheragāthā

* Ekakanipāta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Puṇṇattheragāthā

(4.)

11\. _“Sabbhireva samāsetha,_  
_paṇḍitehatthadassibhi;_  
_Atthaṁ mahantaṁ gambhīraṁ,_  
_duddasaṁ nipuṇaṁ aṇuṁ;_  
_Dhīrā samadhigacchanti,_  
_appamattā vicakkhaṇā”ti._  

12\. Itthaṁ sudaṁ āyasmā puṇṇo mantāṇiputto thero gāthaṁ abhāsitthāti.
