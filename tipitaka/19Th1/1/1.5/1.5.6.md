# 1.5.6 Samiddhittheragāthā

* Ekakanipāta

* Pañcamavagga

* Samiddhittheragāthā

(46.)

105\. _“Saddhāyāhaṁ pabbajito,_  
_Agārasmānagāriyaṁ;_  
_Sati paññā ca me vuḍḍhā,_  
_Cittañca susamāhitaṁ;_  
_Kāmaṁ karassu rūpāni,_  
_Neva maṁ byādhayissasī”ti._
