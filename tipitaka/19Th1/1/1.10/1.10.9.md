# 1.10.9 Uttiyattheragāthā

* Ekakanipāta

* Dasamavagga

* Uttiyattheragāthā

(99.)

221\. _“Saddaṁ sutvā sati muṭṭhā,_  
_Piyaṁ nimittaṁ manasikaroto;_  
_Sārattacitto vedeti,_  
_Tañca ajjhosa tiṭṭhati;_  
_Tassa vaḍḍhanti āsavā,_  
_Saṁsāraṁ upagāmino”ti._  

222\. … Uttiyo thero… .
