# 2.2.1 Mahācundattheragāthā

* Dukanipāta

* Dutiyavagga

* Mahācundattheragāthā

(141.)

306\. _“Sussūsā sutavaddhanī,_  
_sutaṁ paññāya vaddhanaṁ;_  
_Paññāya atthaṁ jānāti,_  
_ñāto attho sukhāvaho._  

(142.)

307\. _Sevetha pantāni senāsanāni,_  
_Careyya saṁyojanavippamokkhaṁ;_  
_Sace ratiṁ nādhigaccheyya tattha,_  
_Saṁghe vase rakkhitatto satimā”ti._
