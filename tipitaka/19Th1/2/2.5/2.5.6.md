# 2.5.6 Cūḷakattheragāthā

* Dukanipāta

* Pañcamavagga

* Cūḷakattheragāthā

(211.)

417\. _“Nadanti morā susikhā supekhuṇā,_  
_Sunīlagīvā sumukhā sugajjino;_  
_Susaddalā cāpi mahāmahī ayaṁ,_  
_Subyāpitambu suvalāhakaṁ nabhaṁ._  

(212.)

418\. _Sukallarūpo sumanassa jhāyataṁ,_  
_Sunikkamo sādhu subuddhasāsane;_  
_Susukkasukkaṁ nipuṇaṁ sududdasaṁ,_  
_Phusāhi taṁ uttamamaccutaṁ padan”ti._  

419\. … Cūḷako thero… .
