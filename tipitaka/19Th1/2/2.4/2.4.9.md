# 2.4.9 Usabhattheragāthā

* Dukanipāta

* Catutthavagga

* Usabhattheragāthā

(197.)

394\. _“Ambapallavasaṅkāsaṁ,_  
_aṁse katvāna cīvaraṁ;_  
_Nisinno hatthigīvāyaṁ,_  
_gāmaṁ piṇḍāya pāvisiṁ._  

(198.)

395\. _Hatthikkhandhato oruyha,_  
_saṁvegaṁ alabhiṁ tadā;_  
_Sohaṁ ditto tadā santo,_  
_patto me āsavakkhayo”ti._
