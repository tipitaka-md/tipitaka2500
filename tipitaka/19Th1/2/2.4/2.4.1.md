# 2.4.1 Migasirattheragāthā

* Dukanipāta

* Catutthavagga

* Migasirattheragāthā

(181.)

370\. _“Yato ahaṁ pabbajito,_  
_sammāsambuddhasāsane;_  
_Vimuccamāno uggacchiṁ,_  
_kāmadhātuṁ upaccagaṁ._  

(182.)

371\. _Brahmuno pekkhamānassa,_  
_tato cittaṁ vimucci me;_  
_Akuppā me vimuttīti,_  
_sabbasaṁyojanakkhayā”ti._  

372\. … Migasiro thero… .
