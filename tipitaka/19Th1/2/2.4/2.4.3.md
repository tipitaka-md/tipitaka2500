# 2.4.3 Upavāṇattheragāthā

* Dukanipāta

* Catutthavagga

* Upavāṇattheragāthā

(185.)

376\. _“Arahaṁ sugato loke,_  
_vātehābādhiko muni;_  
_Sace uṇhodakaṁ atthi,_  
_munino dehi brāhmaṇa._  

(186.)

377\. _Pūjito pūjaneyyānaṁ,_  
_sakkareyyāna sakkato;_  
_Apacitopaceyyānaṁ,_  
_tassa icchāmi hātave”ti._  

378\. … Upavāṇo thero… .
