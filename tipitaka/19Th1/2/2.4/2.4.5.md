# 2.4.5 Sambulakaccānattheragāthā

* Dukanipāta

* Catutthavagga

* Sambulakaccānattheragāthā

(189.)

382\. _“Devo ca vassati, devo ca gaḷagaḷāyati,_  
_Ekako cāhaṁ bherave bile viharāmi;_  
_Tassa mayhaṁ ekakassa bherave bile viharato,_  
_Natthi bhayaṁ vā chambhitattaṁ vā lomahaṁso vā._  

(190.)

383\. _Dhammatā mamesā yassa me,_  
_Ekakassa bherave bile;_  
_Viharato natthi bhayaṁ vā,_  
_Chambhitattaṁ vā lomahaṁso vā”ti._  

384\. … Sambulakaccāno thero… .
