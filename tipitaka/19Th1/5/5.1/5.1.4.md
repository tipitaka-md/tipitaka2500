# 5.1.4 Sumanattheragāthā

* Pañcakanipāta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Sumanattheragāthā

(330.)

585\. _“Yaṁ patthayāno dhammesu,_  
_upajjhāyo anuggahi;_  
_Amataṁ abhikaṅkhantaṁ,_  
_kataṁ kattabbakaṁ mayā._  

(331.)

586\. _Anuppatto sacchikato,_  
_sayaṁ dhammo anītiho;_  
_Visuddhañāṇo nikkaṅkho,_  
_byākaromi tavantike._  

(332.)

587\. _Pubbenivāsaṁ jānāmi,_  
_dibbacakkhu visodhitaṁ;_  
_Sadattho me anuppatto,_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanaṁ._  

(333.)

588\. _Appamattassa me sikkhā,_  
_sussutā tava sāsane;_  
_Sabbe me āsavā khīṇā,_  
_natthi dāni punabbhavo._  

(334.)

589\. _Anusāsi maṁ ariyavatā,_  
_Anukampi anuggahi;_  
_Amogho tuyhamovādo,_  
_Antevāsimhi sikkhito”ti._  

590\. … Sumano thero… .
