# 3.1.4 Dhaniyattheragāthā

* Tikanipāta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Dhaniyattheragāthā

(228.)

446\. _“Sukhañce jīvituṁ icche,_  
_sāmaññasmiṁ apekkhavā;_  
_Saṁghikaṁ nātimaññeyya,_  
_cīvaraṁ pānabhojanaṁ._  

(229.)

447\. _Sukhañce jīvituṁ icche,_  
_sāmaññasmiṁ apekkhavā;_  
_Ahi mūsikasobbhaṁva,_  
_sevetha sayanāsanaṁ._  

(230.)

448\. _Sukhañce jīvituṁ icche,_  
_sāmaññasmiṁ apekkhavā;_  
_Itarītarena tusseyya,_  
_ekadhammañca bhāvaye”ti._  

449\. … Dhaniyo thero… .
