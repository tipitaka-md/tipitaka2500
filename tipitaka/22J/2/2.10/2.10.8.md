# 2.10.8 Kiṃsukopamajātaka

* Dukanipāta

* Siṅgālavagga

* Kiṃsukopamajātaka

(196.)

656\. _“Sabbehi kiṁsuko diṭṭho,_  
_kinnvettha vicikicchatha;_  
_Na hi sabbesu ṭhānesu,_  
_sārathī paripucchito”._  

(197.)

657\. _“Evaṁ sabbehi ñāṇehi,_  
_yesaṁ dhammā ajānitā;_  
_Te ve dhammesu kaṅkhanti,_  
_kiṁsukasmiṁva bhātaro”ti._  

658\. Kiṁsukopamajātakaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
