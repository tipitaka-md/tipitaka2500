# 2.4.8 Sīhakotthujātaka

* Dukanipāta

* Asadisavagga

* Sīhakotthujātaka

(75.)

459\. _“Sīhaṅgulī sīhanakho,_  
_sīhapādapatiṭṭhito;_  
_So sīho sīhasaṁghamhi,_  
_eko nadati aññathā”._  

(76.)

460\. _“Mā tvaṁ nadi rājaputta,_  
_appasaddo vane vasa;_  
_Sarena kho taṁ jāneyyuṁ,_  
_na hi te pettiko saro”ti._  

461\. Sīhakotthujātakaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
