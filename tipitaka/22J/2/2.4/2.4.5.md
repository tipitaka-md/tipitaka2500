# 2.4.5 Anabhiratijātaka

* Dukanipāta

* Asadisavagga

* Anabhiratijātaka

(69.)

450\. _“Yathodake āvile appasanne,_  
_Na passati sippikasambukañca;_  
_Sakkharaṁ vālukaṁ macchagumbaṁ,_  
_Evaṁ āvilamhi citte;_  
_Na passati attadatthaṁ paratthaṁ._  

(70.)

451\. _Yathodake acche vippasanne,_  
_So passati sippikasambukañca;_  
_Sakkharaṁ vālukaṁ macchagumbaṁ,_  
_Evaṁ anāvilamhi citte;_  
_So passati attadatthaṁ paratthan”ti._  

452\. Anabhiratijātakaṁ pañcamaṁ.
