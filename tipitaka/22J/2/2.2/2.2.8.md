# 2.2.8 Sakuṇagghijātaka

* Dukanipāta

* Santhavavagga

* Sakuṇagghijātaka

(35.)

395\. _“Seno balasā patamāno,_  
_lāpaṁ gocaraṭhāyinaṁ;_  
_Sahasā ajjhappattova,_  
_maraṇaṁ tenupāgami._  

(36.)

396\. _Sohaṁ nayena sampanno,_  
_pettike gocare rato;_  
_Apetasattu modāmi,_  
_sampassaṁ atthamattano”ti._  

397\. Sakuṇagghijātakaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
