# 2.2.6 Upasāḷakajātaka

* Dukanipāta

* Santhavavagga

* Upasāḷakajātaka

(31.)

389\. _“Upasāḷakanāmāni,_  
_sahassāni catuddasa;_  
_Asmiṁ padese daḍḍhāni,_  
_natthi loke anāmataṁ._  

(32.)

390\. _Yamhi saccañca dhammo ca,_  
_ahiṁsā saṁyamo damo;_  
_Etaṁ ariyā sevanti,_  
_etaṁ loke anāmatan”ti._  

391\. Upasāḷakajātakaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
