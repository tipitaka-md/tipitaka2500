# 2.8.3 Puṭabhattajātaka

* Dukanipāta

* Kāsāvavagga

* Puṭabhattajātaka

(145.)

576\. _“Name namantassa bhaje bhajantaṁ,_  
_Kiccānukubbassa kareyya kiccaṁ;_  
_Nānatthakāmassa kareyya atthaṁ,_  
_Asambhajantampi na sambhajeyya._  

(146.)

577\. _Caje cajantaṁ vanathaṁ na kayirā,_  
_Apetacittena na sambhajeyya;_  
_Dijo dumaṁ khīṇaphalanti ñatvā,_  
_Aññaṁ samekkheyya mahā hi loko”ti._  

578\. Puṭabhattajātakaṁ tatiyaṁ.
