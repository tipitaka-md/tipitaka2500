# 2.3.6 Kaḷāyamuṭṭhijātaka

* Dukanipāta

* Kalyāṇavagga

* Kaḷāyamuṭṭhijātaka

(51.)

421\. _“Bālo vatāyaṁ dumasākhagocaro,_  
_Paññā janinda nayimassa vijjati;_  
_Kaḷāyamuṭṭhiṁ avakiriya kevalaṁ,_  
_Ekaṁ kaḷāyaṁ patitaṁ gavesati”._  

(52.)

422\. _“Evameva mayaṁ rāja,_  
_ye caññe atilobhino;_  
_Appena bahuṁ jiyyāma,_  
_kaḷāyeneva vānaro”ti._  

423\. Kaḷāyamuṭṭhijātakaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
