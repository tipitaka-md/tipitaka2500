# 2.1.4 Uragajātaka

* Dukanipāta

* Daḷhavagga

* Uragajātaka

(7.)

349\. _“Idhūragānaṁ pavaro paviṭṭho,_  
_Selassa vaṇṇena pamokkhamicchaṁ;_  
_Brahmañca vaṇṇaṁ apacāyamāno,_  
_Bubhukkhito no vitarāmi bhottuṁ”._  

(8.)

350\. _“So brahmagutto cirameva jīva,_  
_Dibyā ca te pātubhavantu bhakkhā;_  
_Yo brahmavaṇṇaṁ apacāyamāno,_  
_Bubhukkhito no vitarāsi bhottun”ti._  

351\. Uragajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.
