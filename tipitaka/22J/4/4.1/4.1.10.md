# 4.1.10 Seyyajātaka

* Catukkanipāta

* Kāliṅgavagga

* Seyyajātaka

(37.)

928\. _“Sasamuddapariyāyaṁ,_  
_mahiṁ sāgarakuṇḍalaṁ;_  
_Na icche saha nindāya,_  
_evaṁ seyya vijānahi._  

(38.)

929\. _Dhiratthu taṁ yasalābhaṁ,_  
_dhanalābhañca brāhmaṇa;_  
_Yā vutti vinipātena,_  
_adhammacaraṇena vā._  

(39.)

930\. _Api ce pattamādāya,_  
_anagāro paribbaje;_  
_Sāyeva jīvikā seyyo,_  
_yā cādhammena esanā._  

(40.)

931\. _Api ce pattamādāya,_  
_anagāro paribbaje;_  
_Aññaṁ ahiṁsayaṁ loke,_  
_api rajjena taṁ varan”ti._  

932\. Seyyajātakaṁ dasamaṁ. Kāliṅgavaggo paṭhamo.

933\. 

934\. _Vivarañca adeyya samiddhavaraṁ,_  
_Atha daddara pāpamahātiraho;_  
_Atha koli palāsavarañca kara,_  
_Carimaṁ sasamuddavarena dasāti._
