# 5.2.4 Khajjopanakajātaka

* Pañcakanipāta

* Vaṇṇārohavagga

* Khajjopanakajātaka

(75.)

1235\. _“Ko nu santamhi pajjote,_  
_aggipariyesanaṁ caraṁ;_  
_Addakkhi ratti khajjotaṁ,_  
_jātavedaṁ amaññatha._  

(76.)

1236\. _Svassa gomayacuṇṇāni,_  
_abhimatthaṁ tiṇāni ca;_  
_Viparītāya saññāya,_  
_nāsakkhi pajjaletave._  

(77.)

1237\. _Evampi anupāyena,_  
_atthaṁ na labhate migo;_  
_Visāṇato gavaṁ dohaṁ,_  
_yattha khīraṁ na vindati._  

(78.)

1238\. _Vividhehi upāyehi,_  
_atthaṁ papponti māṇavā;_  
_Niggahena amittānaṁ,_  
_mittānaṁ paggahena ca._  

(79.)

1239\. _Senāmokkhapalābhena,_  
_vallabhānaṁ nayena ca;_  
_Jagatiṁ jagatipālā,_  
_āvasanti vasundharan”ti._  

1240\. Khajjopanakajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.
