# 1.2.6 Tipallatthamigajātaka

* Ekakanipāta

* Sīlavagga

* Tipallatthamigajātaka

(16.)

34\. _“Migaṁ tipallatthamanekamāyaṁ,_  
_Aṭṭhakkhuraṁ aḍḍharattāpapāyiṁ;_  
_Ekena sotena chamāssasanto,_  
_Chahi kalāhitibhoti bhāgineyyo”ti._  

35\. Tipallatthamigajātakaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
