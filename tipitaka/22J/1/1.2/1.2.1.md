# 1.2.1 Lakkhaṇamigajātaka

* Ekakanipāta

* Sīlavagga

* Lakkhaṇamigajātaka

(11.)

24\. _“Hoti sīlavataṁ attho,_  
_paṭisanthāravuttinaṁ;_  
_Lakkhaṇaṁ passa āyantaṁ,_  
_ñātisaṅghapurakkhataṁ;_  
_Atha passasimaṁ kāḷaṁ,_  
_suvihīnaṁva ñātibhī”ti._  

25\. Lakkhaṇamigajātakaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
