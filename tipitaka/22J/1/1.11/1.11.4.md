# 1.11.4 Mittavindakajātaka

* Ekakanipāta

* Parosatavagga

* Mittavindakajātaka

(104.)

231\. _“Catubbhi aṭṭhajjhagamā,_  
_aṭṭhāhipi ca soḷasa;_  
_Soḷasāhi ca bāttiṁsa,_  
_atricchaṁ cakkamāsado;_  
_Icchāhatassa posassa,_  
_cakkaṁ bhamati matthake”ti._  

232\. Mittavindakajātakaṁ catutthaṁ.
