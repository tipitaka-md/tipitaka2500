# 1.11.5 Dubbalakaṭṭhajātaka

* Ekakanipāta

* Parosatavagga

* Dubbalakaṭṭhajātaka

(105.)

233\. _“Bahumpetaṁ vane kaṭṭhaṁ,_  
_vāto bhañjati dubbalaṁ;_  
_Tassa ce bhāyasī nāga,_  
_kiso nūna bhavissasī”ti._  

234\. Dubbalakaṭṭhajātakaṁ pañcamaṁ.
