# 1.10.6 Telapattajātaka

* Ekakanipāta

* Littavagga

* Telapattajātaka

(96.)

212\. _“Samatittikaṁ anavasekaṁ,_  
_Telapattaṁ yathā parihareyya;_  
_Evaṁ sacittamanurakkhe,_  
_Patthayāno disaṁ agatapubban”ti._  

213\. Telapattajātakaṁ chaṭṭhaṁ.
