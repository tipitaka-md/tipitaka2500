# 12.8 Kālattheravatthu

* Attavagga

* Kālattheravatthu

(164.)

176\. _Yo sāsanaṁ arahataṁ,_  
_ariyānaṁ dhammajīvinaṁ;_  
_Paṭikkosati dummedho,_  
_diṭṭhiṁ nissāya pāpikaṁ;_  
_Phalāni kaṭṭhakasseva,_  
_attaghātāya phallati._
