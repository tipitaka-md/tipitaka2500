# 4.5 Macchariyakosiyaseṭṭhivatthu

* Pupphavagga

* Macchariyakosiyaseṭṭhivatthu

(49.)

53\. _Yathāpi bhamaro pupphaṁ,_  
_vaṇṇagandhamaheṭhayaṁ;_  
_Paleti rasamādāya,_  
_evaṁ gāme munī care._
