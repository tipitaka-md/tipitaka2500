# 4.1 Pathavikathāpasutapañcasatabhikkhuvatthu

* Pupphavagga

* Pathavikathāpasutapañcasatabhikkhuvatthu

(44.)

48\. _Ko imaṁ pathaviṁ vicessati,_  
_Yamalokañca imaṁ sadevakaṁ;_  
_Ko dhammapadaṁ sudesitaṁ,_  
_Kusalo pupphamiva pacessati._  

(45.)

49\. _Sekho pathaviṁ vicessati,_  
_Yamalokañca imaṁ sadevakaṁ;_  
_Sekho dhammapadaṁ sudesitaṁ,_  
_Kusalo pupphamiva pacessati._
