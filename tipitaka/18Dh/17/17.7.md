# 17.7 Atulaupāsakavatthu

* Kodhavagga

* Atulaupāsakavatthu

(227.)

246\. _Porāṇametaṁ atula,_  
_netaṁ ajjatanāmiva;_  
_Nindanti tuṇhimāsīnaṁ,_  
_nindanti bahubhāṇinaṁ;_  
_Mitabhāṇimpi nindanti,_  
_natthi loke anindito._  

(228.)

247\. _Na cāhu na ca bhavissati,_  
_na cetarahi vijjati;_  
_Ekantaṁ nindito poso,_  
_ekantaṁ vā pasaṁsito._  

(229.)

248\. _Yañce viññū pasaṁsanti,_  
_anuvicca suve suve;_  
_Acchiddavuttiṁ medhāviṁ,_  
_paññāsīlasamāhitaṁ._  

(230.)

249\. _Nikkhaṁ jambonadasseva,_  
_ko taṁ ninditumarahati;_  
_Devāpi naṁ pasaṁsanti,_  
_brahmunāpi pasaṁsito._
