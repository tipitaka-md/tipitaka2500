# 8.6 Sāriputtattherassa bhāgineyyavatthu

* Sahassavagga

* Sāriputtattherassa bhāgineyyavatthu

(107.)

115\. _Yo ca vassasataṁ jantu,_  
_aggiṁ paricare vane;_  
_Ekañca bhāvitattānaṁ,_  
_muhuttamapi pūjaye;_  
_Sāyeva pūjanā seyyo,_  
_yañce vassasataṁ hutaṁ._
