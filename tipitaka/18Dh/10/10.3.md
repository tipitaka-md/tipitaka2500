# 10.3 Sambahulakumārakavatthu

* Daṇḍavagga

* Sambahulakumārakavatthu

(131.)

141\. _Sukhakāmāni bhūtāni,_  
_yo daṇḍena vihiṁsati;_  
_Attano sukhamesāno,_  
_pecca so na labhate sukhaṁ._  

(132.)
