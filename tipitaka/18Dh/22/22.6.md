# 22.6 Issāpakataitthivatthu

* Nirayavagga

* Issāpakataitthivatthu

(314.)

338\. _Akataṁ dukkaṭaṁ seyyo,_  
_pacchā tappati dukkaṭaṁ;_  
_Katañca sukataṁ seyyo,_  
_yaṁ katvā nānutappati._
