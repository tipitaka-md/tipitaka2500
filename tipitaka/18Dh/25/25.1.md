# 25.1 Pañcabhikkhuvatthu

* Bhikkhuvagga

* Pañcabhikkhuvatthu

(360.)

387\. _Cakkhunā saṁvaro sādhu,_  
_sādhu sotena saṁvaro;_  
_Ghānena saṁvaro sādhu,_  
_sādhu jivhāya saṁvaro._  

(361.)

388\. _Kāyena saṁvaro sādhu,_  
_sādhu vācāya saṁvaro;_  
_Manasā saṁvaro sādhu,_  
_sādhu sabbattha saṁvaro;_  
_Sabbattha saṁvuto bhikkhu,_  
_sabbadukkhā pamuccati._
