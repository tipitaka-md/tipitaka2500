# 14.6 Aggidattabrāhmaṇavatthu

* Buddhavagga

* Aggidattabrāhmaṇavatthu

(188.)

202\. _Bahuṁ ve saraṇaṁ yanti,_  
_pabbatāni vanāni ca;_  
_Ārāmarukkhacetyāni,_  
_manussā bhayatajjitā._  

(189.)

203\. _Netaṁ kho saraṇaṁ khemaṁ,_  
_netaṁ saraṇamuttamaṁ;_  
_Netaṁ saraṇamāgamma,_  
_sabbadukkhā pamuccati._  

(190.)

204\. _Yo ca buddhañca dhammañca,_  
_saṁghañca saraṇaṁ gato;_  
_Cattāri ariyasaccāni,_  
_sammappaññāya passati._  

(191.)

205\. _Dukkhaṁ dukkhasamuppādaṁ,_  
_dukkhassa ca atikkamaṁ;_  
_Ariyaṁ caṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ,_  
_dukkhūpasamagāminaṁ._  

(192.)

206\. _Etaṁ kho saraṇaṁ khemaṁ,_  
_etaṁ saraṇamuttamaṁ;_  
_Etaṁ saraṇamāgamma,_  
_sabbadukkhā pamuccati._
