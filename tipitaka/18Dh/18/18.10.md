# 18.10 Meṇḍakaseṭṭhivatthu

* Malavagga

* Meṇḍakaseṭṭhivatthu

(252.)

272\. _Sudassaṁ vajjamaññesaṁ,_  
_attano pana duddasaṁ;_  
_Paresaṁ hi so vajjāni,_  
_opunāti yathā bhusaṁ;_  
_Attano pana chādeti,_  
_kaliṁva kitavā saṭho._
