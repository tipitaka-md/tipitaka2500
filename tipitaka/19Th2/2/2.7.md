# 2.7 Mittātherīgāthā

* Dukanipāta

* Mittātherīgāthā

(31.)

57\. _“Cātuddasiṁ pañcadasiṁ,_  
_yā ca pakkhassa aṭṭhamī;_  
_Pāṭihāriyapakkhañca,_  
_aṭṭhaṅgasusamāgataṁ._  

(32.)

58\. _Uposathaṁ upāgacchiṁ,_  
_devakāyābhinandinī;_  
_Sājja ekena bhattena,_  
_muṇḍā saṅghāṭipārutā;_  
_Devakāyaṁ na patthehaṁ,_  
_vineyya hadaye daran”ti._  

59\. … Mittā therī… .
