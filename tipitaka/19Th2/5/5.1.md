# 5.1 Aññataratherīgāthā

* Pañcakanipāta

* Aññataratherīgāthā

(67.)

109\. _“Paṇṇavīsativassāni,_  
_yato pabbajitā ahaṁ;_  
_Nāccharāsaṅghātamattampi,_  
_cittassūpasamajjhagaṁ._  

(68.)

110\. _Aladdhā cetaso santiṁ,_  
_kāmarāgenavassutā;_  
_Bāhā paggayha kandantī,_  
_vihāraṁ pāvisiṁ ahaṁ._  

(69.)

111\. _Sā bhikkhuniṁ upāgacchiṁ,_  
_yā me saddhāyikā ahu;_  
_Sā me dhammamadesesi,_  
_khandhāyatanadhātuyo._  

(70.)

112\. _Tassā dhammaṁ suṇitvāna,_  
_ekamante upāvisiṁ;_  
_Pubbenivāsaṁ jānāmi,_  
_dibbacakkhu visodhitaṁ._  

(71.)

113\. _Cetopariccañāṇañca,_  
_sotadhātu visodhitā;_  
_Iddhīpi me sacchikatā,_  
_patto me āsavakkhayo;_  
_Chaḷabhiññā sacchikatā,_  
_kataṁ buddhassa sāsanan”ti._  

114\. … Aññatarā therī… .
