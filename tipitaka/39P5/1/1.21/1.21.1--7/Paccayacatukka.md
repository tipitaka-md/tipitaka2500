288\. Hetuyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ. (Saṁkhittaṁ.)

289\. (Sahajātavārampi…pe… pañhāvārampi vitthāretabbaṁ.)

(85.)

290\. Kusalaṁ anidassanaappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca kusalo anidassanaappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. 293\. Kusalaṁ anidassanaappaṭighañca abyākataṁ anidassanaappaṭighañca dhammaṁ paṭicca abyākato anidassanaappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (1)
