# 4.1.1 Suddhikasutta

* Indriyasaṃyutta

* Suddhikavagga

* Suddhikasutta

(471.)

1000\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. Tatra kho bhagavā etadavoca. “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, indriyāni. Katamāni pañca? Saddhindriyaṁ, vīriyindriyaṁ, satindriyaṁ, samādhindriyaṁ, paññindriyaṁ— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcindriyānī”ti.

1001\. Paṭhamaṁ.
