# 11.6.1 Sagāthakasutta

* Sotāpattisaṃyutta

* Sappaññavagga

* Sagāthakasutta

(1047.)

2091\. “Catūhi, bhikkhave, dhammehi samannāgato ariyasāvako sotāpanno hoti avinipātadhammo niyato sambodhiparāyaṇo.

2092\. Katamehi catūhi? Idha, bhikkhave, ariyasāvako buddhe aveccappasādena samannāgato hoti— itipi so bhagavā…pe… satthā devamanussānaṁ buddho bhagavāti. Dhamme…pe… saṁghe…pe… ariyakantehi sīlehi samannāgato hoti akhaṇḍehi…pe… samādhisaṁvattanikehi. Imehi kho, bhikkhave, catūhi dhammehi samannāgato ariyasāvako sotāpanno hoti avinipātadhammo niyato sambodhiparāyaṇo”ti. Idamavoca bhagavā. Idaṁ vatvāna sugato athāparaṁ etadavoca satthā—

2093\. _“Yassa saddhā tathāgate,_  
_acalā suppatiṭṭhitā;_  
_Sīlañca yassa kalyāṇaṁ,_  
_ariyakantaṁ pasaṁsitaṁ._  

2094\. _Saṁghe pasādo yassatthi,_  
_ujubhūtañca dassanaṁ;_  
_Adaliddoti taṁ āhu,_  
_amoghaṁ tassa jīvitaṁ._  

2095\. _Tasmā saddhañca sīlañca,_  
_pasādaṁ dhammadassanaṁ;_  
_Anuyuñjetha medhāvī,_  
_saraṁ buddhānasāsanan”ti._  

2096\. Paṭhamaṁ.
