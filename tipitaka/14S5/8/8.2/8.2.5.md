# 8.2.5 Ṭhānasutta

* Anuruddhasaṃyutta

* Dutiyavagga

* Ṭhānasutta

(913.)

1601\. “Imesañca panāhaṁ, āvuso, catunnaṁ satipaṭṭhānānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā ṭhānañca ṭhānato aṭṭhānañca aṭṭhānato yathābhūtaṁ pajānāmī”ti.

1602\. Pañcamaṁ.
