# 8.2.14 Āsavakkhayasutta

* Anuruddhasaṃyutta

* Dutiyavagga

* Āsavakkhayasutta

(922.)

1619\. “Imesañca panāhaṁ, āvuso, catunnaṁ satipaṭṭhānānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā āsavānaṁ khayā anāsavaṁ cetovimuttiṁ paññāvimuttiṁ diṭṭheva dhamme sayaṁ abhiññā sacchikatvā upasampajja viharāmī”ti.

1620\. Cuddasamaṁ.

1621\. Dutiyo vaggo.

1622\. 

1623\. _Mahābhiññaṁ iddhi dibbaṁ,_  
_Cetopariyaṁ ṭhānaṁ kammaṁ;_  
_Sabbatthadhātudhimutti,_  
_Indriyaṁ jhānaṁ tisso vijjāti._  

1624\. Anuruddhasaṁyuttaṁ aṭṭhamaṁ.
