# 8.2.2 Iddhividhasutta

* Anuruddhasaṃyutta

* Dutiyavagga

* Iddhividhasutta

(910.)

1595\. “Imesañca panāhaṁ, āvuso, catunnaṁ satipaṭṭhānānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā anekavihitaṁ iddhividhaṁ paccanubhomi— ekopi hutvā bahudhā homi…pe… yāva brahmalokāpi kāyena vasaṁ vattemī”ti.

1596\. Dutiyaṁ.
