# 8.2.10 Indriyaparopariyattasutta

* Anuruddhasaṃyutta

* Dutiyavagga

* Indriyaparopariyattasutta

(918.)

1611\. “Imesañca panāhaṁ, āvuso, catunnaṁ satipaṭṭhānānaṁ bhāvitattā bahulīkatattā parasattānaṁ parapuggalānaṁ indriyaparopariyattaṁ yathābhūtaṁ pajānāmī”ti.

1612\. Dasamaṁ.
