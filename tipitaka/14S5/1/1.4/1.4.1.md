# 1.4.1 Paṭhamapaṭipattisutta

* Maggasaṃyutta

* Paṭipattivagga

* Paṭhamapaṭipattisutta

(31.)

117\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Micchāpaṭipattiñca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi, sammāpaṭipattiñca. Taṁ suṇātha. Katamā ca, bhikkhave, micchāpaṭipatti? Seyyathidaṁ— micchādiṭṭhi…pe… micchāsamādhi. Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, micchāpaṭipatti. Katamā ca, bhikkhave, sammāpaṭipatti? Seyyathidaṁ— sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāsamādhi. Ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sammāpaṭipattī”ti.

118\. Paṭhamaṁ.
