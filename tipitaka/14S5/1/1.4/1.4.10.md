# 1.4.10 Dutiyabrahmacariyasutta

* Maggasaṃyutta

* Paṭipattivagga

* Dutiyabrahmacariyasutta

(40.)

141\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Brahmacariyañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi, brahmacariyatthañca. Taṁ suṇātha. Katamañca, bhikkhave, brahmacariyaṁ? Ayameva ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo, seyyathidaṁ— sammādiṭṭhi…pe… sammāsamādhi. Idaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, brahmacariyaṁ. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, brahmacariyattho? Yo kho, bhikkhave, rāgakkhayo dosakkhayo mohakkhayo— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, brahmacariyattho”ti.

142\. Dasamaṁ.

143\. Paṭipattivaggo catuttho.

144\. 

145\. _Paṭipatti paṭipanno ca,_  
_viraddhañca pāraṅgamā;_  
_Sāmaññena ca dve vuttā,_  
_brahmaññā apare duve;_  
_Brahmacariyena dve vuttā,_  
_vaggo tena pavuccatīti._
