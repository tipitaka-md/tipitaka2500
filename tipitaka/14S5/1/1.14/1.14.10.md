# 1.14.10 Uddhambhāgiyasutta

* Maggasaṃyutta

* Oghavagga

* Uddhambhāgiyasutta

(181.)

353\. “Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, uddhambhāgiyāni saṁyojanāni. Katamāni pañca? Rūparāgo, arūparāgo, māno, uddhaccaṁ, avijjā— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṁyojanāni. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ uddhambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo. Katamo ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sammādiṭṭhiṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ…pe… sammāsamādhiṁ bhāveti vivekanissitaṁ virāganissitaṁ nirodhanissitaṁ vossaggapariṇāmiṁ. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ uddhambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabboti.

354\. Pañcimāni, bhikkhave, uddhambhāgiyāni saṁyojanāni. Katamāni pañca? Rūparāgo, arūparāgo, māno, uddhaccaṁ, avijjā— imāni kho, bhikkhave, pañcuddhambhāgiyāni saṁyojanāni. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ uddhambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo. Katamo ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo? Idha, bhikkhave, bhikkhu sammādiṭṭhiṁ bhāveti…pe… sammāsamādhiṁ bhāveti rāgavinayapariyosānaṁ dosavinayapariyosānaṁ mohavinayapariyosānaṁ… amatogadhaṁ amataparāyanaṁ amatapariyosānaṁ… nibbānaninnaṁ nibbānapoṇaṁ nibbānapabbhāraṁ. Imesaṁ kho, bhikkhave, pañcannaṁ uddhambhāgiyānaṁ saṁyojanānaṁ abhiññāya pariññāya parikkhayāya pahānāya ayaṁ ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo bhāvetabbo”ti.

355\. Dasamaṁ.

356\. Oghavaggo aṭṭhamo.

357\. 

358\. _Ogho yogo upādānaṁ,_  
_ganthaṁ anusayena ca;_  
_Kāmaguṇā nīvaraṇaṁ,_  
_khandhā oruddhambhāgiyāti._  

359\. 

360\. _Avijjāvaggo paṭhamo,_  
_dutiyaṁ vihāraṁ vuccati;_  
_Micchattaṁ tatiyo vaggo,_  
_catutthaṁ paṭipanneneva._  

361\. _Titthiyaṁ pañcamo vaggo,_  
_chaṭṭho sūriyena ca;_  
_Bahukate sattamo vaggo,_  
_uppādo aṭṭhamena ca._  

362\. _Divasavaggo navamo,_  
_Dasamo appamādena ca;_  
_Ekādasabalavaggo,_  
_Dvādasa esanā pāḷiyaṁ;_  
_Oghavaggo bhavati terasāti._  

363\. Maggasaṁyuttaṁ paṭhamaṁ.
