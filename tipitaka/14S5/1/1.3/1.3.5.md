# 1.3.5 Paṭhamaasappurisasutta

* Maggasaṃyutta

* Micchattavagga

* Paṭhamaasappurisasutta

(25.)

98\. Sāvatthinidānaṁ. “Asappurisañca vo, bhikkhave, desessāmi, sappurisañca. Taṁ suṇātha. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, asappuriso? Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco micchādiṭṭhiko hoti, micchāsaṅkappo, micchāvāco, micchākammanto, micchāājīvo, micchāvāyāmo, micchāsati, micchāsamādhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, asappuriso.

99\. Katamo ca, bhikkhave, sappuriso? Idha, bhikkhave, ekacco sammādiṭṭhiko hoti, sammāsaṅkappo, sammāvāco, sammākammanto, sammāājīvo, sammāvāyāmo, sammāsati, sammāsamādhi— ayaṁ vuccati, bhikkhave, sappuriso”ti.

100\. Pañcamaṁ.
