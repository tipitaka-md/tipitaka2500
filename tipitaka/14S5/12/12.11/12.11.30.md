# 12.11.30 Pettidevapettivisayasutta

* Saccasaṃyutta

* Pañcagatipeyyālavagga

* Pettidevapettivisayasutta

(1201.)

2545\. … “Evameva kho, bhikkhave, appakā te sattā ye pettivisayā cutā devesu paccājāyanti; atha kho eteva bahutarā sattā ye pettivisayā cutā pettivisaye paccājāyanti. Taṁ kissa hetu? Adiṭṭhattā, bhikkhave, catunnaṁ ariyasaccānaṁ. Katamesaṁ catunnaṁ? Dukkhassa ariyasaccassa, dukkhasamudayassa ariyasaccassa, dukkhanirodhassa ariyasaccassa, dukkhanirodhagāminiyā paṭipadāya ariyasaccassa.

2546\. Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, ‘idaṁ dukkhan’ti yogo karaṇīyo, ‘ayaṁ dukkhasamudayo’ti yogo karaṇīyo, ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodho’ti yogo karaṇīyo, ‘ayaṁ dukkhanirodhagāminī paṭipadā’ti yogo karaṇīyo”ti.

2547\. Idamavoca bhagavā. Attamanā te bhikkhū bhagavato bhāsitaṁ abhinandunti.

2548\. Tiṁsatimaṁ.

2549\. Pañcagatipeyyālavaggo ekādasamo.

2550\. 

2551\. _Manussato cutā chāpi,_  
_devā cutā nirayato;_  
_Tiracchānapettivisayā,_  
_tiṁsamatto gativaggoti._  

2552\. Saccasaṁyuttaṁ dvādasamaṁ.

2553\. Mahāvaggo pañcamo.

2554\. 

2555\. _Maggabojjhaṅgaṁ satiyā,_  
_indriyaṁ sammappadhānaṁ;_  
_Baliddhipādānuruddhā,_  
_jhānānāpānasaṁyutaṁ;_  
_Sotāpatti saccañcāti,_  
_mahāvaggoti vuccatīti._  

2556\. Mahāvaggasaṁyuttapāḷi niṭṭhitā.

2557\. Saṁyuttanikāyo samatto.
