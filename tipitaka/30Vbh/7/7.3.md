# 7.3 Pañhāpucchaka

* Satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅga

* Pañhāpucchaka

* Satipaṭṭhānavibhaṅga

* Pañhāpucchaka

(386.)

1382\. Cattāro satipaṭṭhānā— idha bhikkhu kāye kāyānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ, vedanāsu vedanānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ, citte cittānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ, dhammesu dhammānupassī viharati ātāpī sampajāno satimā vineyya loke abhijjhādomanassaṁ.

(387.)
