# 16.2.6 Chakkaniddesa

* Ñāṇavibhaṅga

* Niddesa

* Chakkaniddesa

(805.)

2547\. Tattha katamā chasu abhiññāsu paññā? Iddhividhe ñāṇaṁ, sotadhātuvisuddhiyā ñāṇaṁ, paracitte ñāṇaṁ, pubbenivāsānussatiyā ñāṇaṁ, sattānaṁ cutūpapāte ñāṇaṁ, āsavānaṁ khaye ñāṇaṁ— imā chasu abhiññāsu paññā. Evaṁ chabbidhena ñāṇavatthu.

2548\. Chakkaṁ.
