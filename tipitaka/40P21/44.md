# 44 Lokiyaduka, Sanidassanattika

* Lokiyaduka, Sanidassanattika

(92.)

235\. Nalokiyaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca lokiyo sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ.

236\. Nalokuttaraṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca lokiyo sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ.

237\. (Gaṇitakena gaṇetabbā tīṇi pañhā. Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

238\. Nalokiyaṁ naanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca lokiyo anidassanasappaṭigho… . (Tīṇiyeva pañhā kātabbā. Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… aññamaññe ekaṁ…pe… avigate tīṇi.

239\. Nalokuttaraṁ naanidassanaappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca lokiyo anidassanaappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekaṁ, adhipatiyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.
