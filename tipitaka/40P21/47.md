# 47 Sāsavaduka, Sanidassanattika

* Sāsavaduka, Sanidassanattika

(95.)

249\. Nasāsavaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sāsavo sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ.

250\. Naanāsavaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sāsavo sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

251\. (Nasāsavaṁ naanidassanasappaṭighamūlassa purimanayena tīṇi pañhā.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… aññamaññe ekaṁ…pe… avigate tīṇi.

252\. Naanāsavaṁ naanidassanaappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sāsavo anidassanaappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekaṁ, adhipatiyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.
