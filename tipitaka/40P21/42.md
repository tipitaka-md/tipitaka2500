# 42 Sappaṭighaduka, Sanidassanattika

* Sappaṭighaduka, Sanidassanattika

(90.)

227\. Nasappaṭighaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sappaṭigho sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ.

228\. Naappaṭighaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sappaṭigho sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ.

229\. Nasappaṭighaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighañca naappaṭighaṁ nasanidassanasappaṭighañca dhammaṁ paṭicca sappaṭigho sanidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā… ekaṁ. (Sabbattha tīṇi.) Hetuyā tīṇi, adhipatiyā tīṇi…pe… avigate tīṇi.

230\. Nasappaṭighaṁ naanidassanasappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca sappaṭigho anidassanasappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekaṁ, adhipatiyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.

231\. Naappaṭighaṁ naanidassanaappaṭighaṁ dhammaṁ paṭicca appaṭigho anidassanaappaṭigho dhammo uppajjati hetupaccayā. (Saṁkhittaṁ.) Hetuyā ekaṁ, adhipatiyā ekaṁ…pe… avigate ekaṁ.
