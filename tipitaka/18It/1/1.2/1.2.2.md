# 1.2.2 Kodhapariññāsutta

* Ekakanipāta

* Dutiyavagga

* Kodhapariññāsutta

(12.)

61\. Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

62\. “Kodhaṁ, bhikkhave, anabhijānaṁ aparijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ avirājayaṁ appajahaṁ abhabbo dukkhakkhayāya. Kodhañca kho, bhikkhave, abhijānaṁ parijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ virājayaṁ pajahaṁ bhabbo dukkhakkhayāyā”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

63\. _“Yena kodhena kuddhāse,_  
_sattā gacchanti duggatiṁ;_  
_Taṁ kodhaṁ sammadaññāya,_  
_pajahanti vipassino;_  
_Pahāya na punāyanti,_  
_imaṁ lokaṁ kudācanan”ti._  

64\. Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

65\. Dutiyaṁ.
