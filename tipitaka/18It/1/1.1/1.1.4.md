# 1.1.4 Kodhasutta

* Ekakanipāta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Kodhasutta

(4.)

17\. Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

18\. “Ekadhammaṁ, bhikkhave, pajahatha; ahaṁ vo pāṭibhogo anāgāmitāya. Katamaṁ ekadhammaṁ? Kodhaṁ, bhikkhave, ekadhammaṁ pajahatha; ahaṁ vo pāṭibhogo anāgāmitāyā”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

19\. _“Yena kodhena kuddhāse,_  
_sattā gacchanti duggatiṁ;_  
_Taṁ kodhaṁ sammadaññāya,_  
_pajahanti vipassino;_  
_Pahāya na punāyanti,_  
_imaṁ lokaṁ kudācanan”ti._  

20\. Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

21\. Catutthaṁ.
