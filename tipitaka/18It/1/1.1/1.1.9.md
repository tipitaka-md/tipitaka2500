# 1.1.9 Lobhapariññāsutta

* Ekakanipāta

* Paṭhamavagga

* Lobhapariññāsutta

(9.)

43\. Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

44\. “Lobhaṁ, bhikkhave, anabhijānaṁ aparijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ avirājayaṁ appajahaṁ abhabbo dukkhakkhayāya. Lobhañca kho, bhikkhave, abhijānaṁ parijānaṁ tattha cittaṁ virājayaṁ pajahaṁ bhabbo dukkhakkhayāyā”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

45\. _“Yena lobhena luddhāse,_  
_sattā gacchanti duggatiṁ;_  
_Taṁ lobhaṁ sammadaññāya,_  
_pajahanti vipassino;_  
_Pahāya na punāyanti,_  
_imaṁ lokaṁ kudācanan”ti._  

46\. Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

47\. Navamaṁ.
