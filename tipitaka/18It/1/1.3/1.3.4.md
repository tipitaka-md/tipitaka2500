# 1.3.4 Aṭṭhipuñjasutta

* Ekakanipāta

* Tatiyavagga

* Aṭṭhipuñjasutta

(24.)

134\. Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

135\. “Ekapuggalassa, bhikkhave, kappaṁ sandhāvato saṁsarato siyā evaṁ mahā aṭṭhikaṅkalo aṭṭhipuñjo aṭṭhirāsi yathāyaṁ vepullo pabbato, sace saṁhārako assa, sambhatañca na vinasseyyā”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

136\. _“Ekassekena kappena,_  
_puggalassaṭṭhisañcayo;_  
_Siyā pabbatasamo rāsi,_  
_iti vuttaṁ mahesinā._  

137\. _So kho panāyaṁ akkhāto,_  
_vepullo pabbato mahā;_  
_Uttaro gijjhakūṭassa,_  
_magadhānaṁ giribbaje._  

138\. _Yato ca ariyasaccāni,_  
_sammappaññāya passati;_  
_Dukkhaṁ dukkhasamuppādaṁ,_  
_dukkhassa ca atikkamaṁ;_  
_Ariyañcaṭṭhaṅgikaṁ maggaṁ,_  
_dukkhūpasamagāminaṁ._  

139\. _Sa sattakkhattuṁ paramaṁ,_  
_sandhāvitvāna puggalo;_  
_Dukkhassantakaro hoti,_  
_sabbasaṁyojanakkhayā”ti._  

140\. Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

141\. Catutthaṁ.
