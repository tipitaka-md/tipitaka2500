# 3.2.2 Cakkhusutta

* Tikanipāta

* Dutiyavagga

* Cakkhusutta

(61.)

393\. Vuttañhetaṁ bhagavatā vuttamarahatāti me sutaṁ—

394\. “Tīṇimāni, bhikkhave, cakkhūni. Katamāni tīṇi? Maṁsacakkhu, dibbacakkhu, paññācakkhu— imāni kho, bhikkhave, tīṇi cakkhūnī”ti. Etamatthaṁ bhagavā avoca. Tatthetaṁ iti vuccati—

395\. _“Maṁsacakkhu dibbacakkhu,_  
_paññācakkhu anuttaraṁ;_  
_Etāni tīṇi cakkhūni,_  
_akkhāsi purisuttamo._  

396\. _Maṁsacakkhussa uppādo,_  
_maggo dibbassa cakkhuno;_  
_Yato ñāṇaṁ udapādi,_  
_paññācakkhu anuttaraṁ;_  
_Yassa cakkhussa paṭilābhā,_  
_sabbadukkhā pamuccatī”ti._  

397\. Ayampi attho vutto bhagavatā, iti me sutanti.

398\. Dutiyaṁ.
