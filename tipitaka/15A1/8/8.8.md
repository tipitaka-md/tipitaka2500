# 8.8

* Kalyāṇamittādivagga

(78.)

164\. “Appamattikā esā, bhikkhave, parihāni yadidaṁ bhogaparihāni. Etaṁ patikiṭṭhaṁ, bhikkhave, parihānīnaṁ yadidaṁ paññāparihānī”ti.

165\. Aṭṭhamaṁ.
