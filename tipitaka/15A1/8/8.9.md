# 8.9

* Kalyāṇamittādivagga

(79.)

166\. “Appamattikā esā, bhikkhave, vuddhi yadidaṁ bhogavuddhi. Etadaggaṁ, bhikkhave, vuddhīnaṁ yadidaṁ paññāvuddhi. Tasmātiha, bhikkhave, evaṁ sikkhitabbaṁ— ‘paññāvuddhiyā vaddhissāmā’ti. Evañhi vo, bhikkhave, sikkhitabban”ti.

167\. Navamaṁ.
