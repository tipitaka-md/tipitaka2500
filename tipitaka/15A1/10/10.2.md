# 10.2

* Dutiyapamādādivagga

(99.)

208\. “Ajjhattikaṁ, bhikkhave, aṅganti karitvā nāññaṁ ekaṅgampi samanupassāmi yaṁ evaṁ mahato atthāya saṁvattati yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, appamādo. Appamādo, bhikkhave, mahato atthāya saṁvattatī”ti.

209\. Dutiyaṁ.
