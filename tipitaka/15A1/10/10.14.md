# 10.14

* Dutiyapamādādivagga

(111.)

217\. “Bāhiraṁ, bhikkhave, aṅganti karitvā nāññaṁ ekaṅgampi samanupassāmi yaṁ evaṁ mahato atthāya saṁvattati yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, kalyāṇamittatā. Kalyāṇamittatā, bhikkhave, mahato atthāya saṁvattatī”ti.

218\. Cuddasamaṁ.
