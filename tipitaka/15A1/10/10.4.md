# 10.4

* Dutiyapamādādivagga

(101.)

212\. “Ajjhattikaṁ, bhikkhave, aṅganti karitvā nāññaṁ ekaṅgampi samanupassāmi yaṁ evaṁ mahato atthāya saṁvattati yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, vīriyārambho. Vīriyārambho, bhikkhave, mahato atthāya saṁvattatī”ti.

213\. Catutthaṁ.
