# 1.4

* Rūpādivagga

(4.)

9\. “Nāhaṁ, bhikkhave, aññaṁ ekarasampi samanupassāmi yaṁ evaṁ purisassa cittaṁ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, itthiraso. Itthiraso, bhikkhave, purisassa cittaṁ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī”ti.

10\. Catutthaṁ.
