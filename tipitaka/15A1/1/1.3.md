# 1.3

* Rūpādivagga

(3.)

7\. “Nāhaṁ, bhikkhave, aññaṁ ekagandhampi samanupassāmi yaṁ evaṁ purisassa cittaṁ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati yathayidaṁ, bhikkhave, itthigandho. Itthigandho, bhikkhave, purisassa cittaṁ pariyādāya tiṭṭhatī”ti.

8\. Tatiyaṁ.
