# 1.1.5 Saṃkhittadhanasutta

* Paṭhamapaṇṇāsaka

* Dhanavagga

* Saṃkhittadhanasutta

(5.)

25\. “Sattimāni, bhikkhave, dhanāni. Katamāni satta? Saddhādhanaṁ, sīladhanaṁ, hirīdhanaṁ, ottappadhanaṁ, sutadhanaṁ, cāgadhanaṁ, paññādhanaṁ. Imāni kho, bhikkhave, satta dhanānīti.

26\. _Saddhādhanaṁ sīladhanaṁ,_  
_hirī ottappiyaṁ dhanaṁ;_  
_Sutadhanañca cāgo ca,_  
_paññā ve sattamaṁ dhanaṁ._  

27\. _Yassa ete dhanā atthi,_  
_itthiyā purisassa vā;_  
_Adaliddoti taṁ āhu,_  
_amoghaṁ tassa jīvitaṁ._  

28\. _Tasmā saddhañca sīlañca,_  
_Pasādaṁ dhammadassanaṁ;_  
_Anuyuñjetha medhāvī,_  
_Saraṁ buddhāna sāsanan”ti._  

29\. Pañcamaṁ.
